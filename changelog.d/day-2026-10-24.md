### Added

- `bench/shot_to_load.py job --no-draw-face-on FIRST-LAST=WHY`: on a short span where the body model's head is wrong,
  no face lines are drawn in `<Video 1>` while the capture and the mask stay as they are.
- `bench/shot_to_load.py job --no-head-on FIRST-LAST=WHY` with `--no-head-form hold|blank`: on such a span the grey
  head itself is held from the last good frame before it, or blanked, with no face marks drawn.
- `marks_against_the_body_model` in the reference step: each frame's head marks checked against the pose table (nose
  distance and downward pitch), printed as data at `reference` and `pair`.
- `bench/flags_before_render.py`: every pre-render flag, carved cell and held or emptied frame of a capture in one
  printout, and `--initial WHO` to record who read it. `bench/load_chain.py render` refuses until an initial newer than
  the capture's status is recorded. `bench/flag_census.py` counts each flag kind across every capture on disk.
- `bench/said_on_shot.py`: every answer and remark the owner has given on a shot, oldest first, with `--for-question`
  for the block that goes under a question. Review pages take `look` items: a contact sheet, the owner's earlier words
  on the shot and their own buttons.
- `preview` defaults `--cast` to the video's cast file.
- Two documents: the procedure for verifying a load against the node's own written files and the laid keeps
  (`docs/masked_v2v/verifying_raw_frames_and_keeps.md`), and the dated record of why the lead's face did not change on
  shot 2050 (`docs/research/masking/2026-10-25_context_frames_and_identity_on_2050.md`), with the perception models page
  brought up to the chain.

### Changed

- The three-path window rule: a window never shows the original subject unmasked. A shot up to 345 frames runs masked
  end to end across its legal window and is laid short; a longer shot runs as chained windows in one load; across a hard
  cut the padding is held copies of the shot's edge. The lane's rules pages cite it and say what they used to claim.
- `check_pass2_source` refuses a joined cut older than a laid row on the shot, and a source cut that already holds the
  load's own subject there.
- The reference crop box is sized over valid and filled frames together when `--fill-from-kept` replaces frames.

### Fixed

- The head seed's gallery search no longer stops at an occluded head; it sizes against the next headed frame.
- A face-only part keeps the beard under the jaw by `JAW_BEARD_PAD_FRAC` (0.35) of the face height inside the person
  track, whatever class the part model gave it, with hands and arms crossing the chin excluded.
- The mesh chin fallback runs only on a frontal pose, so a closed mouth is no longer drawn on the cheek on profile frames.
- One `name_taken` across every jobs folder, so a stem taken in an earlier day's folder is skipped at job creation.
- Checks resolve ComfyUI from a worktree nested under `custom_nodes`.
