### Added

- A tracked builder for masked jobs, `bench/masked_job.py` over
  `workflows/masked_job_graph.py`: from a job file, `build preview`,
  `build plan`, `build render`, `queue` and `collect`. A job is built on its
  video's analysis folder and a capture that has passed `verify`, and a
  render is queued only behind the capture's gate. A load's `motion` is
  `mesh` or `none` and its `render_region` is `frame` or `part`. A text in
  a job file is its file, the frames of one shot it was written from and
  its writer: a load its text does not cover, a text that holds a cut, the
  same words under two labels, and a plan or a render for a load nobody
  has looked at with that text are refused, so a load that holds a cut is
  not built. The other keys and every refusal are in the docstring, the
  defaults in `workflows/h3_config.py` (`MASKED_JOB_*`), and the account
  in `docs/wiki/masked_v2v.md`, "Running a job".
- `clip_folder.py` and `bench/clip_analysis.py init`: an input video's
  analysis folder, `data/<stem>/`, found by file name and never by path,
  with a manifest, `verify()` against the file's recorded identity and a
  `fingerprint` over what a render reads (`bench/check_clip_folder.py`).
- `bench/cast_file.py` keeps a person's answer about a tile of a shot table
  as a frame and a box, and writes the Subject Track's `seeds` text.
  `bench/who_is_who_across_shots.py` reads the trackers' shot tables of a
  load and writes a row per shot, `answered` or `ask`, for the gate.
- `MiniMaxH3SubjectTrack`: optional `seeds`, a line per shot that names the
  subject by a frame and a box, or `none`.
- `MiniMaxH3MaskedSource`: `render_region`, `the whole frame` or
  `zoomed in on what is replaced`. A zoomed load is rendered in a single
  box at the canvas's size (`video_mask.ZOOM_LARGEST` is the limit) and laid
  back into the whole frame; it takes no wired motion video and neither
  continues a run nor is continued. `bench/assemble_delivery.py` lays a
  piece at the source's size from the zoomed picture written beside it.
- `bench/capture_masked_run.py`: `shots` and `shots --loads` (the gate's
  answer per shot and per load), `rules` (`rules_input.json`), `looked`
  (who looked through a capture's frames), `verify --no-mask` (a written
  statement for a stretch with no mask) and `preflight --who-is-who`.
  `video --job --load` draws a load's strip with its whole text under
  every frame and writes what it showed, and `looked` records it.
- `bench/break_checks.py`: breaks code in a copy, says if a check notices.
- `AGENTS.md`, operative rules: the changelog is written once a day from
  the commits; a rule that depends on the picture takes its value from the
  input video; preprocessing data is verified when it is made, by code and
  by eyes; a record under `bench/results/` is its `.md`; nothing an earlier
  run made is reused by a key; a scene's text is data and a caption, read
  against its frames; a graph posted by hand is a temporary plan whose
  text is assumed wrong until read whole; a capture records who made it
  and who verified it, and when; a wrong render is read first as its
  text, its mask and the input frames, mask frames, sampler frames and
  audio lined up.
- `docs/wiki/who_is_who.md` and `who_is_who_evidence.md`, the design for
  recognising a named subject across cuts. `docs/wiki/decisions.md` holds
  the approved keep, simplify and remove lists for the masked lane, and
  `docs/wiki/handover.md` is the page a session reads first: what the
  last working day left, what it did not, and what is not certain.

### Changed

- `MiniMaxH3SubjectTrack` joins nested detections of one person
  (`subject_tracks.one_each`). With `others` wired, a frame's people are
  everybody the detector found, in a single numbering for every tracker of
  a job: a person an earlier tracker holds is marked
  (`held_by_another_tracker`) and never chosen, and the only-person rule is
  off. The tracker's `MASK_VERSION` moved, so a correction typed against an
  older tile is refused by the job builder until a preview is run again.
- The masked composite never lays a frame that has no mask of its own
  (`video_mask.unlaid_frames`). A window past its source's last frame holds
  that frame with its mask, so the region stays open
  (`video_mask.held_mask`). A zoomed motion reference is framed on the
  replaced part when a part is replaced (`video_mask.window_boxes`).
- `masked_prompt_text.py`: the whole-person and head-and-hair roles no
  longer state what the node cannot know of a clip, nor speak of an edit.
  The soundscape line is held once as `AMBIENCE` and read by
  `workflows/build_workflows.py::_ref_prompt`. The head-and-upper-body role
  keeps its accepted wording, its sentences of the cut kinds held as `HELD`
  until a render with and without them is read. No schema or graph change.
- `bench/capture_masked_run.py`: the gate gained top-level rules, among
  them `subject_never_found`, `load_lays_nothing_on_frames` and
  `who_is_who_not_run`; its docstring lists every line it judges by.
- `bench/assemble_delivery.py`: the record's `ends` fails a row that begins
  or ends on the source's own picture, the shots proof takes its cuts from
  a capture's shot table, and `--alias` reads a capture's label as the
  table's name for a subject.
- Data files under `bench/results/` that no check names moved to the
  untracked `data/results/`; `.gitignore` ignores new data files there.

### Fixed

- The song node counts a track's frames with `loop_plan.track_frames`: a
  track a fraction of a sample over a whole frame count no longer writes a
  frame past its load.

### Removed

- The stores that handed back, by a key, what an earlier run made:
  `mask_store.py` and `reuse_mask` on the Masked Source; `reuse_windows` on
  the song node, with the key functions of `loop_resume.py`;
  `window_keep.py`; and the session store of `reference_encode.py`, with
  `keep_references`. Their checks go with them. Every run renders every
  window, a stored window is read only when `continue_from` names it, and
  the rebuilt shipped graphs no longer name `reuse_windows` or `reuse_mask`.
- The Masked Source's motion choice `a video I wire, zoomed in`, and
  `loop_plan.first_frame_choices`. A graph saved from the canvas with a
  node that lost an input is made again from a shipped graph.
- Added and removed the same day: the Masked Source's `held_tail`,
  `largest_zoom`, `motion_blur_share`, `motion_blur_pixels`, `motion_grey`
  and `motion_box`; the assembler's `restore=<subject>:contested`; and the
  who-is-who tool's `galleries`, `looks` and joint-decision modes.
