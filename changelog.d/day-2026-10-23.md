### Added

- `bench/shot_to_load.py job --margin N --margin-why WHY`: a load's own margin, asked in place of the region's default
  on a person's word, recorded in the job file and never carried to a remake.
- `bench/shot_to_load.py job --hold-part FIRST-LAST@SOURCE`: on a short run of frames where a subject's part is lost or
  mislabeled, the part from a named frame is placed where it stands.
- `bench/shot_to_load.py preview --head-per-stretch WHY`: the tracker follows the head inside each stretch where the
  person is seen, on request; the head tracked over the whole span stays the default. The reason may not contain a
  semicolon.
- `bench/cast_file.py box --encloses head|body`: a box the owner drew by hand on a sheet, entered as approximate. It
  names the detected person mostly inside it, never an outline.
- The plan word `source_facial_hair`: for a face-only subject whose source shows facial hair, the face's own piece
  below the jaw is kept in the region instead of being cut at the head track's last row.
- A closed mouth can be drawn on the lips and named in the text, its bend read from the source's mouth corners where
  they can be read.

### Changed

- The subject tracker node seeds from every owner box in a cut, not only the latest. A drawn box picks the detected
  person mostly inside it, a box prompt must outline one person who is no other subject, and the shot table records the
  code that ran (`ran_code`). Needs a ComfyUI restart.
- A second-subject text names her once, with the face and the hair of `<Picture 1>`. The plan's hair change is retired
  and refused.
- A piece of another person is kept out only where it lies on another detected person; a piece touching her with no
  person of its own is hers. Strangers files made before this are refused, so a remake takes a fresh preview.
- A kept accessory restored from the film is placed on the render's own face, moved frame by frame from one face
  point. A head-worn row with no face points is laid as filmed and flagged.

### Fixed

- The head tube bridges a short dip after a partial recovery, bounded by whole heads and never onto a frame marked
  hidden.
- A semicolon in the `--head-per-stretch` reason split the tracker's seeds text and failed the preview in its first
  node; the preview now refuses it up front.
