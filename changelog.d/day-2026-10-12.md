### Added

- A load of two subjects in one pass, in the masked job builder: a load's
  `subjects`, a list of two, each with its region, still, reference video
  and keep-outs. The Masked Source takes a second reference video
  (`motion_video_2`, `motion_short_edge_2`), shown to the model as
  `<Video 2>`. Nothing ties a still to a place in the picture: the text
  alone tells the model which still is whose. The capture tool reads such a
  load as a load of both: its own plan is worked out from both parts, no
  subject of the load is "another subject" to it, the rules that are facts
  about a subject are read once for each, and the look's strip says which
  `<Subject N>`, `<Picture N>` and `<Video N>` is whose and shows both
  references. A few rules still read the first subject only
  (`docs/wiki/next_steps.md`).
- A hair band under a head-and-hair part (`hair_band` on a load): a
  rectangle under the part is cut out of the clothing keep-out so the
  region's lower edge there holds still. `bench/read_hair_band.py` reads a
  band as figures a frame, for the band load and a load of the same frames
  without it; it prints no verdict.
- The reference video of a head replacement, in one command:
  `bench/head_reference.py` cuts the body model to one box round the head
  (`bench/crop_reference_to_head.py`) and draws the face through the pose
  model's points on it (`bench/draw_face_points.py`). An example job in
  that form is under `bench/examples/masked_job_face_points/`.
- `bench/sweep_loads.py`: copies of masked job files with one named change
  (a still, a text, a reference with its text, a setting), a record of the
  sweep, and the builder's commands in order. It builds and queues nothing.
- `bench/shot_coverage.py`: what a video still needs, a shot and a subject
  at a time, from the shots record, the plan and the delivery table.
- The owner's page of who is in each shot (`bench/who_page.py`), and the
  tools that take the page's answers into records:
  `bench/page_answers_to_cast.py` (a numbered picture's answer into the
  cast file, a plain frame's into the shots record with `--shots`),
  `bench/page_answers_to_verdicts.py` and `bench/page_answers_ledger.py`.
  `bench/renders_page.py` lays every render of a video in frame order with
  the verdict lines under each.
- The song node prints what each reference costs, in a render and in a
  plan: tokens to the text encoder and rows to the video model.

### Changed

- The Body Pose node takes the tracked person's mask, so a box that holds
  two people poses the one tracked.
- The look before a plan or a render is shown and never refused; a look's
  strip is written to the share and not under `data/`.
- `bench/capture_masked_run.py video` stacks a render that begins inside
  its capture's span (a load whose first frames are left as the source, a
  continuation) and names the span's frames it leaves out; it takes the
  source's sound when the render carries none.
- The capture reads the plan of a run that continues a stored window: its
  kept frames are that window's and not a gap.
- `bench/who_is_who_across_shots.py corrections --frames` cuts the shot
  tables of two previews to a load's frames, so two subjects tracked in
  different previews can be given one who-is-who file.
- The body model against the source, in `bench/draw_face_points.py` and
  `bench/draw_lid_marks.py`, is printed and recorded as figures with what
  they were read on; it no longer fails the run. A run passes when the
  file holds what was drawn on the frames it was told.
- `AGENTS.md` is cut to the rules a session cannot work around, and the
  masked lane's page (`docs/wiki/masked_v2v.md`) is reorganized with an
  index and held to the clips and code it names.
- The day's records: the reference form for a face, head or hair
  replacement is written where a session reads it first (`AGENTS.md`,
  `docs/wiki/masked_v2v.md`), with the example job beside it; the
  decisions, the handover and the next steps carry the evening's readings
  (four renders of the second subject and five of the lead in that form,
  the hair band's first three renders) and where the night stopped.

### Fixed

- The lid marks' read-back no longer takes the body model's own
  highlights for marks.
- "No dots" in the face drawing leaves out the points that belong to no
