bump: minor

### Added

- `bench/pick_frames.py`: frames to look at are picked where something changes, with a one-a-second floor, in place of
  fixed sheets of evenly spaced frames.
- `bench/shot_to_load.py preview --for LABEL`: a preview names the replacement it is made for, so the pass-2 source check
  counts only the other replacements laid on the shot; it is required wherever anything is laid there.
- `bench/shot_to_load.py job --eyes-shut-form lowered`: a person's span word that draws her eyes lowered (the iris arc
  between the lids, no disc) on chosen frames.
- `bench/shot_to_load.py text --say-not-used WHY`: test-only, writes the old text form with both "not used" clauses for an
  A/B render; to be retired.

### Changed

- Masked render windows are only lengths on both clocks (`h3_rules.on_both_clocks`), and a shot shorter than its window is
  padded with real source frames before and after it, untouched and tossed after the decode, in place of held copies of
  its last frame. The zoom box covers that context across a cut. A job carries the source's frame count, so a window at
  the clip's end shifts its context or is refused.
- The load text: the kept hair reads "face and blonde hair", and no retention line ends with a "not used" clause.
- The Masked Prompt node writes no negative clauses ("not reused", "not present in the target video"), and
  `bench/examples/` is removed.
- The head tube re-seeds a lost head from the person track only where the subject's own face points show; a face-only
  subject's tube pixels keep their own classes.
- `--no-face-on` drops only the face classes from a hair-and-face part, so her hair stays in the region.
- `bench/laid.py` requires and cites the owner's word for every kept piece; `remove` takes a reason and keeps a record.
- `build_cut_map` shows the cut and its gaps as the laid list has them, with where each came from.
- The lane docs (`docs/masked_v2v/`) point at the code and records, and hold the owner's rules for passes, prep and judging.

### Fixed

- The delivery table honors a laid line's frames, and `laid.py` refuses malformed spans.
- The assembler's audio proof compares decoded samples, so a PCM source no longer fails every cut.
