### Added

- `bench/laid.py` and `delivery_table.py --laid`: one file of what the cut lays (`laid.jsonl`), a line a piece (`shot`,
  `subject`, `load`, `file`, `sha256`, who chose it). `delivery_table.py --laid` lays only those lines in their order,
  refuses `--prefer` and `--hold`, and stops on a file whose bytes or zoomed companion changed, a line whose subject is
  not in its shot, or a render the rule never lets stand. `laid.py seed` writes the list from an existing table;
  `laid.py refs` checks each laid piece's reference against what its render read. Both review pages
  (`bench/review_pages.py`) print the list and its sha256 beside the table's name.
- `bench/frame_coverage.py`: per-frame coverage and gap check across a delivery table's JSON and the captures its rows
  tie to (`run=`). Per shot, subject, and frame it writes the SAM 3.1 track size, the laid piece, the mask its run
  carried there, and a state, listing runs of frames where the original shows while the person is tracked, seams where
  two laid renders of one person meet, frames emptied inside a piece by cause (`drop=`, the floor, the grade, nothing
  saved, the run's no-mask list; `render_ledger.py` now reads the same causes), frames ruled out by a look in the shots
  record (`left out by a look`, while a job's `left_out` stays context), data chips for `laid_but_barely_changed` and
  `region_far_below_its_own_size`, and a per-shot worst-first ranking (`by_shot`: `remake` and `capture`).
- `bench/sidecar.py`: writes `<video file name>.json` beside a video (`<name>.mp4.json`, distinct from a tool's own
  `<stem>.json`) recording how it was made: the video's sha256, the command as run, the commit and uncommitted paths,
  every `H3_` environment key, the tool and inputs with their sha256s, and for a joined clip each laid piece and window
  H3 rendered (its source frames, the text and graph in full, every input held to the queue's digests, the reference
  chain with each drawing's record, the ledger's word, and the job graph held to the PNG saved beside the render).
  `assemble_delivery.py` and `stack_delivery.py` write a sidecar for every clip they build, and `shot_review_clips.py`
  treats a missing or stale sidecar as a hard fault. `what_h3_got.sha256` and `sidecar.sha256_of` share a per-run
  digest cache.
- `bench/frame_table.py` and `load_chain.py table`: one per-frame table beside a capture (`frame_table.json` and `.md`)
  uniting the track and part sizes, the outcome (`replaced`, `held`, `dropped` with the job's reason, `emptied` with
  its cause, `not in the load`), the mask files with their digests, and the gate's flags. Run at the end of
  `load_chain.py on-plan` and as a re-runnable `table` step; `masked_job.py gate` refuses a render whose table is
  missing or stale.
- Exact matte saving and assembly (`video_mask.py`, `audio_freeze_song.py`, `assemble_delivery.py`): `lay_back` and
  `matte_of` return the canvas weight (`uint8`) a window was blended with, and the song node saves that weight beside
  each window's region as `matte` at the whole canvas whatever the zoom, along with `piece` metadata (the output's
  stem, its first piece frame, and how many frames it wrote). A piece whose every window file holds a matte is laid by
  it (`laid_by` rule `"matte"`) without growing or feathering its edge again, and at the source's size adds the render
  delta to the sharp source in the soft band.
- `docs/wiki/masked_v2v_pipeline.md` and `docs/wiki/masked_v2v.md`: an end-to-end data-flow page for the masked
  video-to-video lane from input video to cut and verdicts, and notes on laying a piece by its matte at the source's
  size in one pass in pixels and why a canvas-delta paste would emboss the old subject's edges.

### Changed

- Every masked load is built with the composite `"whole region"` (`h3_config.MASKED_LOAD_COMPOSITE`, passed through
  `masked_job_graph.py` as `composite` and recorded with who set it) in place of `"only what changed"`, so regenerated
  pixels inside a person's region are not restored to the source where they lie close in value. A piece rendered
  `"whole region"` before matte saving is laid by its run's region rather than `changed()`, while giving up pixels
  claimed only by another subject's row.
- Keep-out carving (`capture_masked_run.py`, `load_chain.py`, `masked_job.py`): in the `files` step, every non-person
  keep-out loses the canvas cells that are more than `OWN_TRACK_CELL_SHARE` the replaced subjects' own tracks and every
  pixel of those tracks (`carve_keep_out`), and `flag_runs` blocks on `keep_out_inside_own_track`. At the `on-plan`
  rebuild, `grid_carve` carves keep-outs on the node's own latent steps and token grid (including zoomed boxes),
  remakes each window's predicted region on the carved files (`predict_regions`, route C), remakes the plan's hair band
  file from the carved track (`masked_job.py band`, and `load_chain.py band-again` for loads planned earlier), and
  hashes the masks after the rebuild; `land` holds the render's window regions to the prediction unit for unit.
- `shot_to_load.py` and `capture_masked_run.py` refuse `pieces` (`pieces=largest`, `h3_config.NO_PIECES_ON`) on a load
  whose part holds a face (`face_and_neck` or mouth) or hair, and `shot_to_load.stay_of` refuses a `stay` keep-out,
  retired in favour of naming the subject's clothing in the load's text.
- `load_text.py` writes a subject's clothing from the plan's per-shot `clothing` list (`{first, last, words, by}`, or
  `words: null` with `by` where no clothing shows) as a sentence of the detailed line (`"<Subject N> wears ..."`),
  checking the whole list on read and refusing a load whose frames no entry holds, overlapping entries, or the old
  single string and `clothing_by` keys.
- `delivery_table.py` refuses a plan `restore` span of a subject's own classes without a non-blank `exception` reason,
  and its printed build chain (and `shot_review_clips.py`) runs `frame_coverage.py` between `assemble_delivery.py` and
  `stack_delivery.py`, handing the coverage JSON to the stack as `--coverage`.
- `stack_delivery.py` builds one two-column view (left column at the source's real size: the original, what changed
  edged white with 1 px zoom outlines, and the cut, with text legends between rows; right column: what H3 saw per
  subject—colouring squares the node kept as the original in bright yellow inside the subject's track and a quiet shade
  outside, read by latent step—and the reference), keeps every layout within H.264 level 5.1, muxes with `+faststart`,
  and writes its sidecar after the final mux.
- `shot_to_load.py` never moves or writes into a reference folder any render read (checking both queue records and
  queued graphs), taking the next `_b`, `_c` folder when the span's folder was already read or is not of the current
  form.
- `AGENTS.md` states the pack's broader H3 scope alongside the masked video generation project in hand, and that a
  build runs on its own branch in a worktree and lands from the merge base.

### Fixed

- `frame_coverage.py` counts the changed share in canvas pixels on both sides without scaling the carried region by the
  loader crop's area, reads a capture tied in another shot when it covers the frame, and records `carried_not_known`
  separately from `tracking not known` when the subject's track is known.
- `what_h3_got.py` recognises a keep-out mask that the `on-plan` rebuild grid-carved (route C) rather than failing it
  as a mismatch against the pre-rebuild plan.
