Selective Precision & Component Architecture

MiniMax H3 taps the unnormalized hidden states after Layer 50 of Qwen3-VL 32B to steer its 33B DiT video diffusion transformer. Our strategy applies mixed-precision quantization: compressing the 30.6B language backbone to 4-bit while leaving the entire visual feature extractor completely unquantized.

The Mathematical Reality: The Qwen3-VL 32B Vision Tower is only ~500M parameters (~1.0 GB in BF16)—less than 2% of the entire model footprint. Quantizing it to 4-bit would save under 750 MB of VRAM, but would irreversibly damage spatial resolution, OCR clarity, and video temporal tracking. Keeping it in full BF16 guarantees pristine visual feature extraction.

Hardware Execution Mechanics (RTX 4090 24GB)
1

Host Memory Allocation (load_context)

The 66.7 GB unquantized BF16 model is loaded into CPU system RAM. Zero VRAM is allocated on the RTX 4090 upfront, avoiding CUDA Out-Of-Memory.

2

Sequential Layer Onloading (sequential_targets=['Qwen3VLTextDecoderLayer'])

LLM-Compressor traces the computational graph and onloads one decoder layer at a time to the GPU. For each layer: passes 256 calibration samples, computes AWQ scaling factors (s) and clipping limits (α), packs weights into 4-bit INT4, and moves the layer back to CPU.

3

Peak VRAM Envelope Control

Only 1 layer (~480 MB) + activation buffers (~2.5 GB) reside on the RTX 4090 at any given time. Total peak VRAM during quantization is maintained under ~3.5 GB.

4

Compressed-Tensors Output Serialization

The quantized model is saved to models/qwen3-vl-32b-W4A16-AWQ-H3/ in Marlin-accelerated compressed format ready for instant deployment in ComfyUI / vLLM.
