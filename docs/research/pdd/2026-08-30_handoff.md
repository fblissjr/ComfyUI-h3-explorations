## 1. What is settled, so nobody re-derives it

- **Per-block quantisation sensitivity is not a variable**: 1.086x across 50
  blocks (`2026-08-30_pdd_block_magnitude.json`). There is no sensitive block.
- **Merging PDD into an int8 weight inflates its error**, 0.00942 → 0.01058 at
  strength 1.0, correlating 0.78 with the module's own `||BA||/||W||`
  (`2026-08-30_pdd_quant_interaction.json`). So a strength schedule keyed to
  quantisation turns PDD down where it does the most work.
- **A bake from the bf16 release costs nothing** — 0.00942 at every strength,
  because it quantises once where the run-time path quantises twice. This
  **inverted** `h3_pdd.md`'s blocker section, which had argued *against*
  pre-merging on fidelity grounds. Still deprioritised; the blocker changed,
  not the priority.
- **`unmerged_blocks` costs no memory.** Both arms staged 19995 MB identically;
  the A/B pairs are resident either way because `add_patches` holds them. An
  earlier claim of +19 MB per block is withdrawn.

**`mlp.fc2` is deliberately NOT un-mergeable.** Its forward is never called on
the INT8 path — `MLP.forward` is `linear_input_act(self.fc2, ...)`, which reads
the weight and does the matmul itself — so lifting it removed its LoRA from the
weight patch and applied nothing in its place. It carries the largest single
update in the file. Do not add it back without a patch point that exists.

## 3. The one result, and what it is not

1344x768 × 345, PDD 4-step, all 50 blocks un-merged, `shape_check ok`,
600/600 rows:

    BLOCK axis   0.0065 (b0) to 0.1078 (b7)    16.6x
    SIGMA axis   0.0265 to 0.0321               1.21x
    top     b7 .108, b11 .081, b15 .078, b14 .073, b9 .072
    bottom  b0 .0065, b35 .0075, b34 .0077, b33 .0078, b32 .0083

**Two things it does not say, and both are load-bearing.**

- **It is not where strength MATTERS.** It measures where the delta is large
  *at the block*, not how much reaches the output.
  `2026-08-29_block_propagation.json` shows those run opposite with depth. So
  b7 > b0 here cannot be read as b7 mattering more.
- **The 1.21x sigma figure is confounded and is withdrawn as a schedule
  number.** Sol runs on three of the four captured steps and declines the
  fourth: `end_percent` 0.74 and `percent_to_sigma(0.75)` is 0.8 exactly at
  shift 12, which is the last captured sigma. The delta is `B @ A @ x` and `x`
  comes from the attention above, so part of that spread is a kernel regime
  change. The block axis is unaffected — every block sees the same regime at a
  given step.

## 4. What to do next, in order

1. **Join this capture to a propagation measurement.** That is what answers the
   owner's question, and it needs no new render on the block axis: the
   observation gives per-block delta size, `probe_block_propagation.py` gives
   per-block output impact, and the product is the quantity nobody has.
2. **Re-run the sigma axis with Sol bypassed**, so all four steps share one
   attention regime. One render. Until then there is no schedule number.
3. **Then vary the schedule**, which is the axis the owner says he already
   knows moves PDD. `h3_text_to_video_pdd_manual_sigmas*.json` already ships
   the tail-weighted six-evaluation partition `[8,8,4,4,4,4]` as a
   `ManualSigmas` node — start from that rather than rediscovering it.
4. **Unfinished and cheap: the adaln axis.** `strength` scales the backbone and
   the modulation together through one `add_patches` call. `head_strength`
   exists because the three surfaces are worth ablating apart; that argument
   was never finished for adaln, and the keys are already separable where they
   are assembled.
