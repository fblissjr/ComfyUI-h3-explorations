# FlashGen: what it is, where our settings come from, how upstream runs it

2026-09-26. The owner asked where the manual sigmas and the LoRA strength come
from, what FlashGen is under the hood, and how upstream uses it, so that
FlashGen works as well as it can before any audio refine. Paths under
`coderef/` are the sister checkouts (`docs/wiki/references.md`); the release
copy is the HF download `Beidouqixing/minimax-h3-4step-lora-flashgen`.

## What FlashGen is

- **A 4-step student LoRA** for MiniMax H3 T2VA, rank 64, on `qkv_proj`,
  `out_proj`, `fc1`, `fc2` and `adaln_proj.linear` (the release README).
- **Training:** distribution matching (VSD, no GAN), data-free, on Ascend NPU,
  at 1344x768 and 5.2 s.
- **Deployment:** its `merge_lora_ckpt.py` folds `W + scale * B A` into a full
  checkpoint and writes the schedule into `model_index.json` as
  `_minimax_h3.base_schedule`. Its own wording is "a DMD2 student LoRA", with the
  schedule "matching the training `--dmd2_student_t_list`".
- **The file** holds bare PEFT `lora_A` / `lora_B` pairs with no alpha and no
  metadata. Scale 1.0 means alpha equals rank.

vllm-omni also runs a ModelScope packaging at runtime, with its own filename
and metadata contract (`coderef/vllm-omni/vllm_omni/diffusion/models/minimax_h3/npu/lora.py`).
The HF file does not satisfy that contract, so from HF the merge is the only
route.

## Where each of our settings comes from

| Setting | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| `FLASHGEN_MANUAL_SIGMAS` | The release's `base_schedule` shifted at 12, which is what vllm-omni does to it (below). Inherited through kijai's file header; `bench/check_distill_settings.py` compares the two. |
| `FLASHGEN_STRENGTH` | The publisher's merge scale 1.0. Both of our files carry alpha so that 1.0 means that. |
| `FLASHGEN_SAMPLER` | Euler. Reasoned when set; vllm-omni's step is deterministic Euler at eta 0, so it is confirmed. |
| `FLASHGEN_LORA` | kijai's rank-reduced resize, which keeps most but not all of each delta (`bench/results/2026-09-25_flashgen_lora_conversion.json`). `FLASHGEN_R64_LORA` is the full rank. |
| Sol-Attn on | This repo's default for every video graph. vllm-omni's NPU recipe runs FlashGen dense, with sparse attention dropped. |
| 345 frames | The owner's length. The training length was 5.2 s, and no upstream says anything about longer clips. |

## How upstream runs it

From a survey of vllm-omni, sglang, LightX2V, DiffSynth-Studio, diffusers, the
official repo and the other H3 packs, 2026-09-26. The file:line citations sit
in the survey's report, and the conclusions are here.

- **vllm-omni is the only upstream that runs FlashGen.** It treats
  `base_schedule` as positions and shifts them per stream: video at 12, audio at
  3. Our ManualSigmas match its video sigmas, and core derives the same audio
  sigmas from them through `time_shift_sigma`, as long as the model's shifts
  stay 12/3. Changing `MiniMaxH3SigmaShift` would move the audio and not the
  video.
- **The step rule is deterministic Euler everywhere.** No upstream re-noises a
  few-step H3 student: Turbo, PDD, TaoMate, FastH3 and FlashGen all step Euler
  at eta 0. ComfyUI's `lcm` would re-noise correctly through H3's audio carry,
  but that would depart from every upstream.
- **No guidance.** vllm-omni has no negative branch for H3 at all, and our
  `BasicGuider` matches it.
- **Its native LoRA route is applied at run time, not merged.**
- **Dense attention** in the NPU recipe, as above.
- **T2VA only.** vllm-omni refuses FlashGen on any other task.
- **Not settled:** whether the trainer treated the list as positions or as raw
  sigmas. Every deployment source shifts it. DiffSynth's generic DMD2, which
  shares the trainer's flag names, uses the list raw, but it needs real data,
  and FlashGen says it is data-free.

## What we found: the merge loses most of FlashGen on our checkpoint

`LoraLoaderModelOnly` on an int8 convrot checkpoint merges each layer's delta by
requantizing it (`comfy/ops.py::resolve_cast_module_with_vbar`). FlashGen's
delta is far below one int8 step, so it survives only in expectation, under
rounding noise several times its size. LightX2V's Turbo is smaller still. The
record is `bench/results/2026-09-26_int8_lora_requant.json`.

`MiniMaxH3LoRABranch` (`lora_branch.py`) applies the LoRA at the call instead,
which is also how vllm-omni's native route applies it. The render pair
`h3_probe_t2v_flashgen_r64_4step` (merged) and the shipped
`h3_text_to_video_flashgen` (at the call) isolates it. The first renders of the
at-the-call arm ran as `h3_probe_t2v_flashgen_r64_4step_branch`, the same graph
under a name removed in 0.147.1. `h3_probe_t2v_flashgen_r64_4step_branch_dense`
adds the recipe's dense attention. `bench/flashgen_lora_path_arms.json` is the
run.
