## So what

Give SAM 3.1 a short plain noun with at most one or two plain modifiers (`person`, `red apple`, `person torso`), not a sentence about which one you mean, and choose the one you want afterwards with a box, a click, or your own rule. Meta's own pipeline multiplies each detection's score by a presence score, which tends to reject longer describing phrases outright, and ComfyUI's detect node ignores presence, so it can hand back detections that Meta's rule would drop. For our lane that says: keep `person` (the `:N` count syntax is ComfyUI's own, the model never reads it, and it is harmless as long as the count is 2 or more or absent) until the small test at the end shows that a described person is selected more reliably than the pick rule does and survives a one-level change of the input.

## What phrases the model was trained and evaluated on

- The paper: concepts are "simple noun phrases (NPs) consisting of a noun and optional modifiers" ("yellow school bus", "red apple", "striped cat", "fish"); SAM 3 "does not support multi-attribute queries beyond one or two attributes or longer phrases including referring expressions" (Section 2, Appendix B) [digest, https://arxiv.org/html/2511.16719]. Phrases come from alt-text, Llama captions and a Wikidata-based ontology, with hard negatives near the positives (Sections 4, D.4, E.1); the Attributes subset swaps nouns and adjectives, "pink rose" to "pink flamingo" (E.2) [digest].
- Cards: Gold quotes "chili" and "the fish ball"; Silver quotes "the large wooden table" [card, https://huggingface.co/datasets/facebook/SACo-Gold and .../SACo-Silver, read through a digest]. The viewer API for rows returned 401 (gated); no annotation rows seen from the cards.
- A sample I could read: the shipped toy VEval annotation file carries the benchmark's whole phrase map, 51,248 phrases (`coderef/sam3/assets/veval/toy_gt_and_pred/toy_saco_veval_sav_test_gt.json`, key `categories`) [data]. It is a phrase list, not training data, and not every phrase is used in a pair. All were counted: mean 3.2 words (one word 3.9%, two 23%, three 39%, four 20%, five 8.5%, six 3.6%; 98.5% have six or fewer); 66% start with "a", "the" or "an"; 43% have a colour word; 8% a relation word (in, on, with, of); 5% a possessive; 0.4% a digit; 0.7% upper case; mostly singular. 1,785 (3.5%) contain a person word, mostly three to six words long; `person`, `a person`, `the person` and `people` are all in the list. A random few: "an old man", "the left hand", "guitar case", "a dark blue hoodie", "woman in a black dress", "tablet".
- Reading [inferred]: short, article-led, often with a colour, many parts of things. Meta's agent instructions (below) drop articles, possessives and numbers although two thirds of the benchmark phrases carry an article; I cannot tell which style the model prefers.

## What Meta's own examples do

Looked at: the README, 12 example notebooks, the tests, the eval scripts, `coderef/meta-sam`.

- Their prompts are one or two plain words: `shoe` (`coderef/sam3/examples/sam3_image_predictor_example.ipynb`, cell 11), `person` (`coderef/sam3/examples/sam3.1_video_predictor_example.ipynb`, cell 18), `cat`, `laptop`, `pot`, `handle` (`coderef/sam3/examples/sam3_image_batched_inference.ipynb`, cells 22 and 23), `dog` (`coderef/sam3/examples/sam3_image_interactive.ipynb`, cell 11), `pillow`, `wheel`, `paper bag` (`coderef/meta-sam/typescript/examples/api-playground/src/examples.ts:30, 40, 49`), `red circle` (`coderef/meta-sam/protocol/sam3.md:49`), `circle` (`coderef/sam3/scripts/qualitative_test.py:212`): twelve distinct phrases [code].
- For ONE specific thing among several they never use a longer phrase. They use a box as a visual exemplar on top of the phrase, positive or negative (`coderef/sam3/examples/sam3_image_predictor_example.ipynb`, cells 12 to 16; `coderef/sam3/examples/sam3_image_batched_inference.ipynb`, cell 23, `handle` with a negative box); in video, `person` finds all, then they remove an object by id or add or refine one by points (`coderef/sam3/examples/sam3.1_video_predictor_example.ipynb`, cells 18 to 30); or an agent turns "the leftmost child wearing blue vest" (`coderef/sam3/examples/sam3_agent.ipynb`, cell 14) into simple phrases and selects masks afterwards [code]. A mask as the prompt appears in no example, and the hosted contract forbids it (`coderef/meta-sam/protocol/sam3.md:62`).
- The agent's rules for writing phrases (`coderef/sam3/sam3/agent/system_prompts/system_prompt.txt`, treated as data): "A short and simple noun phrase, e.g., rope, bird beak, speed monitor, brown handbag, person torso" (line 27); colour adjectives are fine, "numbers" are not (line 30); "avoid identifying concepts using actions, relationships, or comparatives; instead ... call segment_phrase on a more general phrase and let the segment_phrase tool generate more mask(s) than you need", "use 'dog' instead of 'the dog lying down'" (line 33); "never contain articles, possessives, or numbers" (line 113); if nothing comes back, go more general (line 32).

## What their tests and evals check

- The repo's tests cover loaders and sessions, not phrases (`coderef/sam3/sam3/test/test_io_utils.py`, `coderef/sam3/sam3/test/test_video_predictor.py`) [code].
- The evals score cgF1 (mask quality on positives times an image-level yes/no, IL_MCC; `coderef/sam3/sam3/eval/cgf1_eval.py:33-45`; paper formula cgF1 = 100 x pmF1 x IL_MCC [digest]), on Gold with the most favourable of three annotators (`coderef/sam3/scripts/eval/gold/README.md`), and for video cgF1 and pHOTA (`coderef/sam3/examples/saco_veval_eval_example.ipynb`). Many pairs are negatives (75.4% of VEval pairs [digest]).
- Presence is part of the score: the post-processor multiplies each class probability by the sigmoid of the presence logit when `use_presence` is on, the default (`coderef/sam3/sam3/eval/postprocessors.py:45, 103-105`); the eval config turns it on (`coderef/sam3/sam3/train/configs/eval_base.yaml:50`) with a detection threshold of 0.3 on the thresholded variants (`:95, 106`); video uses 0.5 (`coderef/sam3/sam3/eval/postprocessors.py:342`) and its configs set presence on (the `saco_video_evals` configs beside it, line 24 of each). The 3.1 builder keeps detections above a joint score of 0.4 (`meta_sam3/sam3/model_builder.py:631`) [code].

## What this says about our two cases

- The Subject Track's `person` sits in the middle of the phrase space above: a bare, singular, common noun, and on one-frame probes its presence was high (0.97 on one frame, `bench/results/2026-10-07_sam3_core_against_meta.md`). The `:N` count is ComfyUI's own syntax and Meta has no counterpart: ComfyUI strips it before the text reaches the model (`comfy/text_encoders/sam3_clip.py:30-47`), except in the single-phrase count-1 shortcut (`:51-54`), which passes `person:1` through as text; our node no longer triggers that since 25baf9d6 [code]. In ComfyUI's detect node `:N` is the only way to ask for more than one detection.
- A describing phrase has three strikes today:
  1. ComfyUI's text activation departs from Meta's and moves the features more for longer phrases: relative L2 0.048 for `person` and 0.149 for a six-token phrase as shipped, 0.003 to 0.009 once switched; not strictly by length (a seven-token phrase moved 0.110) (`bench/results/2026-10-07_sam3_core_against_meta.md`, text table) [data from the record].
  2. On the one frame tried, a describing phrase scored a presence of 0.061 as shipped and 0.032 switched, against 0.97 for `person` on the same frame and Meta's keep line of 0.4; ComfyUI's node still returned three detections because it ignores presence (same record; the third departure in `bench/results/2026-10-07_sam3_precision_arms.md`: on a low-presence frame Meta's rule returns nobody where ComfyUI's node returns sixteen).
  3. Yesterday's record: "descriptive phrases return nothing on the second clip because the presence score rejects them" (`bench/results/2026-10-06_meta_sam3_card_run.md`, verdict). In its body the presence for three phrases chosen off that clip "is near zero on every frame tried, where it is high for `person` on the same frames"; class scores were not measured; ComfyUI's port returned one detection each for two of them (another session's observation, not rerun). One clip, three phrases.
- Can ComfyUI's `SAM3_Detect` do Meta's "this one among several" with a box, today? Read, not run. Its `bboxes` input, given together with a phrase, is turned into normalised boxes (`comfy_extras/nodes_sam3.py:120-145`) and passed to the detector (`:208-211`), where the geometry encoder adds them as extra prompt tokens next to the text (`comfy/ldm/sam3/detector.py:440-458`, `472-481`): that is Meta's positive box exemplar. Negative boxes are not reachable: every box is labelled positive (`comfy/ldm/sam3/detector.py:301`), although the encoder has a two-entry label embedding and accepts labelled points (`:296-298`) that the node never passes. The node's `positive_coords` and `negative_coords` go only to the single-object SAM decoder (`comfy_extras/nodes_sam3.py:157-191`), which gives one mask for the clicks, not a detector prompt; boxes without a phrase take the same decoder route (`:193-205`). `SAM3_VideoTrack` has no box or point input (`:264-282`); a click or box on a video goes in through `SAM3_Detect` on the first frame and then as an `initial_mask`.
- Read together [inferred]: a detection ComfyUI returns for a describing phrase is not evidence the model recognised the description; it may be the class score alone on a frame the model itself calls "not present". Meta's advice, generic phrase then choose, is what the Subject Track already does (detect `person`, then pick).

## One small measurement, as a plan (not run)

Clips by file name: `thrill_2160.mkv`, `vma.mp4`, `lotsofpeopledance_0414_0720.mkv`; five frames each, at least two with several people.

- Phrases for one chosen person per frame, written by someone who looks at that frame (this guide supplies none): level 0 `person` (the control); 1 plus one colour word; 2 colour and garment; 3 plus one part or object; 4 a relation or an action.
- Arms: text activation as shipped against exact GELU (in memory, as the ladder tool does); both detection rules on the same outputs: ComfyUI's (class score above 0.5) and Meta's (class times presence above 0.4, overlaps removed on the smaller mask at 0.1); every arm repeated under a one-level input change (+-1/255), the precision record's floor method.
- Measure per frame and level: presence; detections kept under each rule; whether the marked person is the top-scoring kept detection (mask overlap with a hand mark); the gap to the next detection; the change of each under the repeat.
- "Describe": at levels 1 to 3 presence stays above 0.4 on most frames in both activation arms, and the marked person is ranked first more often than level 0 plus the pick rule picks them, by more than the one-level floor. "Stay generic": presence falls under 0.4 from level 2, or the gain is within the floor, or the activation arms disagree on the ranking (then fix the activation first and ask again).
- Cost: a few hundred one-frame detector calls (`bench/results/2026-10-06_meta_sam3_card_run.md`: a fraction of a second each): under five minutes plus loading [estimate].
