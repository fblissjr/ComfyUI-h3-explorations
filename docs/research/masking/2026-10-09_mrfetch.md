## 1. The late start: what core's first sigmas are, and what a start below the maximum mixes

**Code.** The schedule is `comfy/samplers.py::simple_scheduler` over the
sampling object's table, which `comfy/model_sampling.py::ModelSamplingAV`
fills through `time_snr_shift` at the shift the graph sets. Nothing in that
path rescales the shift by sequence length. The mix at the first step is
`comfy/model_sampling.py::CONST.noise_scaling`: sigma times the noise plus
one minus sigma times the latent, for the video stream and, on the video's
schedule, for the audio stream.

**Measured** (the table's formula evaluated in plain Python, 2026-10-09):
at a video shift of 12 and 16 steps the first six sigmas are 1.0, 0.99452,
0.98824, 0.98119, 0.97297 and 0.96359. A start four knots in therefore
gives each token 2.7 percent of the source's latent.

**Inferred.** That figure is per token. A component of the picture that
spans many tokens (a mean colour, a layout) is averaged over all of them,
so its signal against the noise grows with the square root of how many it
spans. On a trained canvas over a long window a global component is far
above the noise at that sigma. It is the reason the release shifts the
schedule at this size at all. So a start four knots in holds the coarse
picture much harder than the per-token share suggests.

**The test.** None needed for the numbers. For the inference: the same
window at a start two knots in and at four, read by the lag measure.

## 2. Why the last latent frame of a window can let go of the source's look

mrship's arm: the reference video reaches the text encoder only, and the
whole frame starts late from `video_mask.py::soften_subject`'s copy of the
source. On one window the last latent frame took the text's look with
everyone still in place. Stills and a lag measure, one window, not judged.

**Code, ruled out.**

- This pack's window code treats every frame alike under a mask of all
  ones: `video_mask.py::window`, `soften_subject`, `start_zero_tokens` and
  `token_mask` have no case for the end of a window. The one end case is
  the frames held past the source's end, which the Song node reports.
- Core's video VAE encoder is a causal convolution with padding at the
  front only (`comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py`, `encode_temporal`). The tail clip
  of a window is padded with repeats of its last frame and the latents
  those repeats make are dropped; being causal, the repeats do not reach
  the two latents that are kept.

**Code, the candidate.** `soften_subject` takes the mean over the colour
channels, so the late start's latent carries no colour: in this arm the
source's look can only arrive through the text encoder's copy.
`reference_conditioning.py` gives the encoder every twelfth frame, and core
pairs the samples into blocks of two, repeating the last when the count is
odd (`comfy/text_encoders/minimax.py`). A window of the length mrship ran
gives an odd count. Its last block is one frame shown twice, stamped at its
own time, where every other block is two frames half a second apart
stamped at their middle. That frame is inside the window's last latent
frame.

**Inferred.** The only colour evidence for the window's end is a block with
no motion in it, and the grey start supplies none. Structure survives
because the grey latent still carries it. Unverified.

**The test.** A window whose sample count is even has no repeated block;
the next length up on the frame rule is one. If that window holds its look
to the end, this is the cause. *Corrected the same day: the Song node
does not take that length. Of the lengths its `window_frames` accepts
(`audio_freeze_song.py`, the input's tooltip), the two shortest sample to an
even count and the three longer ones all end on a repeated block, so the
test without a code change is the next length down from mrship's, at the
same start, text and seed. No input sets the sampling stride or the count
(`reference_conditioning.py`, where the samples are taken). A test at
mrship's own length needs one more sample at the window's last frame, with
explicit timestamps, which core's tokenizer takes; that is a change to node
code and has not been made.* A cross-check that costs nothing: whether
the same flip shows in the arms where the reference video's rows are on.
If it does, the next suspects are the decode (the last frames of a window
come from the overlap half of the final decode chunk,
`comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py`, `_decode_temporal_frame_plan`) and the source
latent's statistics per latent frame.

**RULED OUT BY RUN, the same evening; kept so it is not found again as
news.** The candidate below (the repeated last block) is not the cause.
**Reported** by mrship from a subagent's four renders on the arm with no
rows and a grey late start, one seed, one render each, read on stills and
measures and not judged: a window with the repeated block and a text that
names no place does not change at its last latent frame; the same graph
with one clause naming a place the window's source never shows does, and
the two graphs differ in that clause alone; a shorter window with an even
count of samples, so no repeated block, changes at its own last latent
frame on the first text; a longer window with an odd count does not. So
the odd block is neither needed nor enough. What breaks through at the end
of the window is a place the text names and the source does not show. Not
separated: the strength of the late start, and why the longest window
holds. The record of those renders is mrship's.

**Later the same day: the repeated block is the release's own
presentation.** Code, read in five implementations, all one rule: every
twelfth frame from the first, the samples paired, an odd count padded by
repeating the last, each block stamped at the mean of its pair's times.

- sglang, the vendor's path:
  `coderef/sglang/python/sglang/multimodal_gen/runtime/pipelines_core/stages/model_specific_stages/minimax_h3/reference_encoding.py::minimax_h3_sample_reference_video_frames`.
  The docstring of its `presentation.py` gives the stamps as a sequence that
  ends on a whole number, which is an odd count ending on a repeated block.
- FastVideo, the trainer:
  `coderef/FastVideo/fastvideo/pipelines/basic/minimax_h3/reference.py::sample_reference_video_frames`,
  whose docstring calls it the released presentation.
- vllm-omni (`vllm_omni/model_executor/models/minimax_h3/reference_video.py`
  in its checkout), LightX2V, and core (`comfy/text_encoders/minimax.py`).

So no upstream samples at another rate, pairs differently or drops the odd
tail, and the model was trained on windows that end this way. If the
repeated block is what lets the last latent frame go, that is the model's
behaviour at that length and not a fault in how this pack samples; sampling
differently would leave the release's presentation. Where the
implementations do differ is the size of the sampled frames (the release
processor's budget against ComfyUI's resize: `video_policy` on the
reference node, `reference_conditioning.py::_configured_qwen_video_frames`)
and whether the video's own rows go with it. The page that already holds
the rule is [`../../h3_references.md`](../../h3_references.md).

**Reported by mrship the same day**, on stills and a lag measure, one
window each, not judged: an arm with the reference video's rows on held the
input's framing through the window and showed no change at the last latent
frame, once its text stopped naming a place the source hides; a late start
one knot in did not show the change and followed the input's movement less
closely than four knots in did. mrship has a subagent on the remaining
arms, the shorter window among them.

## 3. One fraction as the mask over the whole frame

**Code.** With a mask value `m` everywhere, on each step
(`comfy/samplers.py::KSamplerX0Inpaint`,
`comfy/model_base.py::MiniMaxH3.scale_latent_inpaint`, and the row labels
and the velocity scale in `comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py`):

- the model's input is `m` of the running latent plus `1 - m` of the source
  at the reference noise level;
- every row is labelled as if its noise were `m` times the step's sigma;
- the velocity is multiplied by `m`;
- the estimate of the clean latent is `m` of the prediction plus `1 - m` of
  the source.

**Inferred.** The source is present on every step and in the result: the
final latent holds a `1 - m` share of the source's own latent. It is a
crossfade in latent space whose model half was predicted at reduced noise,
not guidance alone. It should hold structure strongly, and it carries the
source's look in the same proportion, which works against an edit of the
look unless the prediction agrees with the source there. The limits on the
value are in [`../../h3_audio_freeze.md`](../../h3_audio_freeze.md)
("Fractional masks are coarse and near-one masks vanish"); mryellow's
reading of a fraction on part of the frame is
[`2026-10-05_mryellow.md`](2026-10-05_mryellow.md), section 1.

**Upstream.** vllm-omni's API takes a single number as the video mask, so it
can do this; its shipped editing graph uses fractions on audio only and
rectangles of zero and one on video. `MaskVidExperiments` has no node for
it. Its soft variant of Differential Diffusion is a different mechanism, a
threshold on a grey mask that moves with the step
(`denoise_mask_function`); on H3 that would change the blend each step and
not the row labels, which core builds from the original mask before
sampling. That mismatch is read, not run.

**The test.** One window at a single fraction beside the late start it is
meant to replace, same seed.

## 4. Later reads the same day, asked for by mrship

All **code**, read in core, in this pack or in a sister checkout. The
renders these answer, their settings and their measures are mrship's and
are recorded by that session, not here; where a result is named it is
**reported**, on stills and measures, not judged.

**Core's own attention node sets the override.**
`comfy_extras/nodes_model_advanced.py::ModelAttentionBackend` calls
`comfy/model_patcher.py::ModelPatcher.set_model_optimized_attention`, which
writes the chosen function to the `optimized_attention_override` key that
`comfy/ldm/modules/attention.py::wrap_attn` tries before it reads a file's
own preference. So a graph that wires that node, with or without Sol, is
not steered by a `comfy_attention` key in a checkpoint or a LoRA; only a
graph with no attention node of any kind is. Its `comfy kitchen attention`
choice is the quantised dense backend, so an arm that wires it is not stock
dense attention. This narrows what
[`../../wiki/references.md`](../../wiki/references.md) says under "ComfyUI
core" for 2026-10-09: "one of our attention nodes" there should be read as
any node that sets the override, core's included.

**Which half of a reference video can go alone.** In core the encoder's
frames always go and the video VAE's rows are the optional half
(`comfy_extras/nodes_minimax_h3.py`: the encoder item is appended, then the
rows only when a VAE is wired). Rows without the encoder's frames cannot be
expressed there, and a search by name of sglang's presentation, vllm-omni
and FastVideo found no switch for it (not an exhaustive read). FastVideo's
Ref2VA training shows every reference with both halves and has no dropout
of either; its model raises on an unconditional pass
(`coderef/FastVideo/fastvideo/train/models/minimax_h3/minimax_h3_ref2va.py`).
That is FastVideo's fine-tune; the base model's own training is not public.

**The size and shape of a reference video upstream.** FastVideo prepares
one with
`coderef/FastVideo/fastvideo/pipelines/basic/minimax_h3/packing.py::resolve_canvas_size`
on the reference's own aspect: the model's short edge, the model's pixel
cap, each side rounded to the canvas multiple. So upstream never shows a
reference video below the canvas's short edge, and keeps the reference's
own shape. A copy at a smaller short edge is outside what that preparation
makes. Where core places a reference whose shape differs from the target's
was not worked through.

**How core decodes a window, and why it is not the cause of a held
frame.** `comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py::MiniMaxH3VideoVAE.decode_temporal`
decodes five latent steps at a time with two more as lookahead and writes
seventeen frames a chunk; the frames the lookahead made are blended into
the first frames of the next chunk. So every window, whatever its length,
has a crossfade of two decodes at the start of each seventeen-frame clip,
and the first frame after a 39-frame context is the first frame after one.
The number of chunks and where the last one falls are the only things that
differ by length; the head of a 141-, a 192- and a 243-frame window is
decoded by the same steps. `audio_freeze.py::window_geometry` gives every
continued window the same context in latent steps, in phase, and the Song
node cuts the same count of frames off each decode. mrship then **reported**
a control that this reading did not survive as an explanation: the change
into each frame, averaged by its place in the seventeen-frame cycle over
six window files, shows no dip at the crossfade, and a held frame moved
when the window plan was swapped. The held frame is not a rule of the
trim, the decode, the writer or the loader.
