# 2026-10-09, mrfetch: three questions from the whole-frame lane, answered from code

last updated: 2026-10-09

mrship, rendering whole-frame video to video on 2026-10-09, asked three
things after the upstream read of that day
([`../../wiki/references.md`](../../wiki/references.md), "What moved by
2026-10-09"). Everything here is **code** (read in core or in this pack) or
**inferred** from it; the one thing **measured** is the arithmetic in
section 1. Nothing was rendered. Each section ends with the test that would
settle it.

## 1. The late start: what core's first sigmas are, and what a start below the maximum mixes

**Code.** The schedule is `comfy/samplers.py::simple_scheduler` over the
sampling object's table, which `comfy/model_sampling.py::ModelSamplingAV`
fills through `time_snr_shift` at the shift the graph sets. Nothing in that
path rescales the shift by sequence length. The mix at the first step is
`comfy/model_sampling.py::CONST.noise_scaling`: sigma times the noise plus
one minus sigma times the latent, for the video stream and, on the video's
schedule, for the audio stream.

**Measured** (the table's formula evaluated in plain Python, 2026-10-09):
at a video shift of 12 and 16 steps the first six sigmas are 1.0, 0.99452,
0.98824, 0.98119, 0.97297 and 0.96359. A start four knots in therefore
gives each token 2.7 percent of the source's latent.

**Inferred.** That figure is per token. A component of the picture that
spans many tokens (a mean colour, a layout) is averaged over all of them,
so its signal against the noise grows with the square root of how many it
spans. On a trained canvas over a long window a global component is far
above the noise at that sigma. It is the reason the release shifts the
schedule at this size at all. So a start four knots in holds the coarse
picture much harder than the per-token share suggests.

**The test.** None needed for the numbers. For the inference: the same
window at a start two knots in and at four, read by the lag measure.

## 2. Why the last latent frame of a window can let go of the source's look

mrship's arm: the reference video reaches the text encoder only, and the
whole frame starts late from `video_mask.py::soften_subject`'s copy of the
source. On one window the last latent frame took the text's look with
everyone still in place. Stills and a lag measure, one window, not judged.

**Code, ruled out.**

- This pack's window code treats every frame alike under a mask of all
  ones: `video_mask.py::window`, `soften_subject`, `start_zero_tokens` and
  `token_mask` have no case for the end of a window. The one end case is
  the frames held past the source's end, which the Song node reports.
- Core's video VAE encoder is a causal convolution with padding at the
  front only (`comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py`, `encode_temporal`). The tail clip
  of a window is padded with repeats of its last frame and the latents
  those repeats make are dropped; being causal, the repeats do not reach
  the two latents that are kept.

**Code, the candidate.** `soften_subject` takes the mean over the colour
channels, so the late start's latent carries no colour: in this arm the
source's look can only arrive through the text encoder's copy.
`reference_conditioning.py` gives the encoder every twelfth frame, and core
pairs the samples into blocks of two, repeating the last when the count is
odd (`comfy/text_encoders/minimax.py`). A window of the length mrship ran
gives an odd count. Its last block is one frame shown twice, stamped at its
own time, where every other block is two frames half a second apart
stamped at their middle. That frame is inside the window's last latent
frame.

**Inferred.** The only colour evidence for the window's end is a block with
no motion in it, and the grey start supplies none. Structure survives
because the grey latent still carries it. Unverified.

**The test.** A window whose sample count is even has no repeated block;
the next length up on the frame rule is one. If that window holds its look
to the end, this is the cause. A cross-check that costs nothing: whether
the same flip shows in the arms where the reference video's rows are on.
If it does, the next suspects are the decode (the last frames of a window
come from the overlap half of the final decode chunk,
`comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py`, `_decode_temporal_frame_plan`) and the source
latent's statistics per latent frame.

## 3. One fraction as the mask over the whole frame

**Code.** With a mask value `m` everywhere, on each step
(`comfy/samplers.py::KSamplerX0Inpaint`,
`comfy/model_base.py::MiniMaxH3.scale_latent_inpaint`, and the row labels
and the velocity scale in `comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py`):

- the model's input is `m` of the running latent plus `1 - m` of the source
  at the reference noise level;
- every row is labelled as if its noise were `m` times the step's sigma;
- the velocity is multiplied by `m`;
- the estimate of the clean latent is `m` of the prediction plus `1 - m` of
  the source.

**Inferred.** The source is present on every step and in the result: the
final latent holds a `1 - m` share of the source's own latent. It is a
crossfade in latent space whose model half was predicted at reduced noise,
not guidance alone. It should hold structure strongly, and it carries the
source's look in the same proportion, which works against an edit of the
look unless the prediction agrees with the source there. The limits on the
value are in [`../../h3_audio_freeze.md`](../../h3_audio_freeze.md)
("Fractional masks are coarse and near-one masks vanish"); mryellow's
reading of a fraction on part of the frame is
[`2026-10-05_mryellow.md`](2026-10-05_mryellow.md), section 1.

**Upstream.** vllm-omni's API takes a single number as the video mask, so it
can do this; its shipped editing graph uses fractions on audio only and
rectangles of zero and one on video. `MaskVidExperiments` has no node for
it. Its soft variant of Differential Diffusion is a different mechanism, a
threshold on a grey mask that moves with the step
(`denoise_mask_function`); on H3 that would change the blend each step and
not the row labels, which core builds from the original mask before
sampling. That mismatch is read, not run.

**The test.** One window at a single fraction beside the late start it is
meant to replace, same seed.

## 4. A thing the masked lane already has

`video_mask.py::window_boxes` and `zoom_plan` plan one fixed box round the
subject for each shot of a window, for the zoomed motion reference. A trial
of rendering a small subject inside a crop, which upstream's
`MaskVidExperiments` does and this lane has not tried, can start from them.
