# Subject, Reference Drawing, and Prompt Recipes (`data/<video>/plan.json` -> `<Video 1>` -> `bench/load_text.py`)

This document is the single reference for how a subject's replacement region, margin, node render zoom, `<Video 1>` 3D head reference crop and facial landmark drawings, and `<Subject 1>` / `<Subject 2>` prompt text are configured in `data/<video>/plan.json`, generated by [`bench/shot_to_load.py`](../../bench/shot_to_load.py), [`bench/head_reference.py`](../../bench/head_reference.py), [`bench/draw_face_points.py`](../../bench/draw_face_points.py), [`bench/crop_reference_to_head.py`](../../bench/crop_reference_to_head.py), and [`bench/load_text.py`](../../bench/load_text.py), and verified by [`bench/flags_before_render.py`](../../bench/flags_before_render.py) before every render.

For the underlying landmark geometry constants and history, see [`docs/masked_v2v/reference_and_prompt_rules.md`](reference_and_prompt_rules.md) and [`docs/masked_v2v/frames_rules.md`](frames_rules.md) (§6–§7).

## 1. General Principles Across All Videos and Subjects

### 1.1 One Recipe Home in `data/<video>/plan.json` (Enforced by `bench/flags_before_render.py`)
Every subject `<s>` in `data/<video>/plan.json` (`subjects.<s>`) declares both its input rules (`region`, `still`, `parts`, `keep_outs`, `text`, `clothing`, `voice`, `reference`, `depth`, `head_marks`) and an explicit `recipe` block (`margin`, `zoom_up_to`, `crop`, `open_mouth`, `closed_mouth`), plus any per-shot exceptions in `shot_overrides`.
- [`bench/shot_to_load.py`](../../bench/shot_to_load.py) reads `subjects.<s>` (and `shot_overrides`) when building `job`, `reference`, and `text`.
- [`bench/flags_before_render.py`](../../bench/flags_before_render.py) (`check_recipe_gate`) checks the built job and reference sidecar against `subjects.<s>.recipe` (merged with any matching `shot_overrides` entry) and refuses pre-render signoff on any mismatch.

### 1.2 Why `closed_mouth: "sculpted"` Is the Universal Baseline for All Subjects
On every frame where Sapiens2 (`sapiens2-seg-1b` + `sapiens2-pose-1b`) classifies the mouth as closed (`(not has_hole) or (gap <= INNER_GAP_CLOSED_PX)` in `bench/draw_face_points.py::INNER_GAP_CLOSED_PX`):
- **`closed_mouth: "sculpted"` (`mouths[f] = None` in `bench/draw_face_points.py::run`)**: draws **no white lines on the lips**, leaving the 3D `SAM3DBody` grey mesh's sculpted closed lips bare.
- **Why `"outlined"` closed mouths fail (`second_hfp_0014i` vs. `second_hfp_0014j`, `second_hfp_0278n` vs. `second_hfp_0278o`)**:
  1. **Visual misreading by H3 (`second_hfp_0014i`)**: Painting a white outer lip ring and center meeting line (`bench/draw_face_points.py::anchored_closed_mouth`) on closed frames produces a bright double loop on `<Video 1>` that H3's video VAE and DiT interpret as a parted/open mouth across closed-mouth frames (`150/154` frames on `14–167`). Switching `second` to `closed_mouth: "sculpted"` (`second_hfp_0014j`) immediately kept her mouth naturally closed and following the source.
  2. **Prompt gate contamination on silent shots (`second_hfp_0278n`)**: In `bench/load_text.py::mouth_drawn` (`bench/load_text.py:214-218`), `mouth_drawn = mouth_drawn_open(drawn, frames) or mouth_drawn_closed(drawn)`. When `closed_mouth` is `"outlined"`, `frames_drawn_closed` is non-empty even when `open_frames == []`, which flips `mouth_drawn` to `True` and injects `TRANSFERRED_DRAWN` (`"and how far the mouth opens"`) and `FOLLOWS_DRAWN` (`"and her mouth opening and closing where its mouth does"`) into a 100% silent shot ([`docs/masked_v2v/2026-10-29_findings_0278n_text_and_frame_table.md`](2026-10-29_findings_0278n_text_and_frame_table.md)). With `closed_mouth: "sculpted"`, `mouth_drawn_closed` is always `False`, so `mouth_drawn` is controlled strictly by whether `open_frames` is non-empty.

### 1.3 Choosing `open_mouth`: `"outlined"` vs. `"inner outline"`
When Sapiens2 detects an open mouth on frame `f` (`f` in `closed_lip_line.open_frames`):
- **`open_mouth: "outlined"` (used for `lead`, the primary vocalist)**:
  - Draws both the outer lip outline and the inner lip outline around the dark mouth interior (`bench/draw_face_points.py::anchored_mouth`), capped to `MOUTH_WIDTH_MOST` and `MOUTH_OPENING_MOST` of the upper face height (`lead_fp_1156d` fix).
  - When `open_frames` is non-empty, `bench/load_text.py::subject_2` appends `MOUTH_CLAUSE["outlined"]` (`"; where the mouth is open, the lips are outlined and it is dark inside, and the mouth opens and closes where the person's mouth does"`), and `retention_analysis` + `[Shot 1]` include the mouth-transfer clauses (plus the singing sentence on voiced spans).
- **`open_mouth: "inner outline"` (used for `second` and `third`)**:
  - Draws a single white outline around the dark inner mouth opening (`inner_lip_ring` filled black) on `open_frames`, fitted to the grey head's mouth-to-chin space (`bench/draw_face_points.py::fit_mouth_to_head`), with no outer lip ring.
  - `<Subject 2>`'s definition stays concise (`FACE_EYES` without `MOUTH_CLAUSE`), while `bench/load_text.py::mouth_drawn_open` still checks `bool(said["open_frames"])`:
    - If `open_frames` is **non-empty** (e.g. `second_hfp_0014j` with `open_frames: [[14, 15], [20, 21]]`, or `third_fp_4912` with `open_frames: [[4913, 4915], [4918, 4921], [4925, 4925], [4930, 4930]]`), `retention_analysis` and `[Shot 1]` include `TRANSFERRED_DRAWN` and `FOLLOWS_DRAWN` so H3 tracks the open-mouth frames.
    - If `open_frames` is **empty (`[]`)** (e.g. `second_hfp_0278o` or `third_fp_4479e`), `mouth_drawn` is `False` and `bench/load_text.py` emits `TRANSFERRED_DRAWN_NO_MOUTH` and `FOLLOWS_DRAWN_NO_MOUTH` (zero mention of the mouth anywhere in the prompt).

### 1.4 `<Video 1>` Reference Crop (`crop: "cranium"`) & Node Render Zoom (`zoom_up_to: 4.0`)
- **`crop: "cranium"` (`bench/crop_reference_to_head.py::cranium_box`, `dc07ccee`)**:
  - Frames `<Video 1>` tightly on the 3D `SAM3DBody` bald grey head (`CRANIUM_ROOM = 0.15` above/beside, `CRANIUM_NECK = 0.35` below the chin, up to `CRANIUM_MOST = 5.75x`) so the drawn dark irises and mouth outlines are large enough for Qwen3-VL (`16x16` patches) and the 3D video VAE.
  - Must always run with `head_track_used: true` (`dc07ccee`, `CRANIUM_HEAD_TRACK_GROW = 0.50`) so a 3D mesh outlier or occluded frame cannot blow up the union crop box and shrink the whole shot's `<Video 1>` head size.
- **`zoom_up_to: 4.0` (`video_mask.py::zoom_box`, `video_mask.py::ZOOM_HEAD_TARGET`, `video_mask.py::ZOOM_MOST_ASKED`)**:
  - Enlarges the DiT render crop toward `ZOOM_HEAD_TARGET = 300 px` up to `4.0x` on distant shots where the subject's head is `< 150 px` tall on the `1024x768` canvas (buying up to `16x` more DiT latent tokens for facial detail), while keeping close-ups (`>= 150 px`) at `<= 2.0x`.

### 1.5 Never Reuse Old Prompts or Old `<Video 1>` References
- Every remake must run `bench/shot_to_load.py reference` and `bench/shot_to_load.py text` fresh from current `data/<video>/plan.json` (never passing `--text` from an older job, never copying an old `.txt` file, and never reusing a pre-`dc07ccee` or pre-recipe `<Video 1>` `.mp4`/`.json`).
- Before queuing any render, read the complete generated prompt with [`bench/what_h3_got.py`](../../bench/what_h3_got.py) (`<load>.render.build_printed.txt`).

<Subject 2> is the movements of the head and the facial expressions performed by the figure in <Video 1>: a closer view of the head and shoulders of a plain grey body model of a person on a black ground, posed as the person moves, with its face drawn as thin white outlines: the eyes, each with a dark iris, open, close and look where the person's eyes do; where the mouth is open, the lips are outlined and it is dark inside, and the mouth opens and closes where the person's mouth does.

detailed_description:
The target video is live-action vintage film from the early 1980s, soft and a little grainy, in warm amber tungsten light.
[Shot 1] <Subject 1>, the girl with red-blonde hair, has the face of <Picture 1>. <Subject 1> performs <Subject 2>: she follows the movements of the head and the facial expressions of the figure, her eyes looking where its eyes look and her mouth opening and closing where its mouth does. <Subject 1> (S1) sings, her mouth opening wide on the sung phrases.

## 3. Pre-Render Verification Checklist for Every Load

Before queuing any render on `:8188`, verify all six checks below:
1. **Zero Prior-Day Inputs Inside Today's Data Files (`--date <today>`)**:
   - Every remake built today must run from a fresh today-dated preview (`data/<today>_shot_c_<shot>_job`), fresh today-dated `strangers.py` / `parts` / `keep_outs` / MoGe-2 `depth.npz`, fresh today-dated `<subj>_head_reference_<first>_<last>.json`, fresh today-dated `.txt` prompt, and fresh today-dated capture (`data/<today>_<load>`).
   - Never reuse a preview, reference, prompt, keep-out, or depth file from a prior day inside a today-dated job or data file.
2. **`<Video 1>` `.head_reference.json` sidecar (Cranium Crop & Drawing Recipe)**:
   - `crop.form == "cranium"` and `crop.cranium.head_track_used == True` (`dc07ccee` active).
   - **Cranium enlargement (`crop.cranium.the_one_box_enlarges` vs. `each_frame_alone_would_enlarge_least_median_most`)**: Verify `the_one_box_enlarges` is not dragged down by detached outlier frames or un-split wide travel (if `the_travel_keeps_of_the_median_frame_enlargement` is low because the subject crosses an occlusion gap, split the load across the occlusion gap or pass `--no-head-on` on `frames_off_the_head_track`).
   - `drawn.iris == "contrast"`, `drawn.mouth.closed_lip_line.open_mouth` is `"outlined"` (`lead`) or `"inner outline"` (`second`, `third`), and `drawn.mouth.closed_lip_line.closed_mouth` is `None` (`"sculpted"` in `plan.json`, meaning zero closed-mouth outlines drawn on `<Video 1>`).
   - **Zero un-excluded `marks_against_the_body_model.flagged_frames`**: Every frame flagged in `marks_against_the_body_model.flagged_frames` (where 2D face landmarks drift off the 3D SMPL-X head mesh) is excluded via `--no-draw-face-on`.
3. **DiT Part-Box Zoom (`zoom_up_to: 4.0` & Active `zoom` in `job.plan.json`)**:
   - Confirm `recipe.zoom_up_to == 4.0` (`--zoom-up-to 4.0`) for all three subjects (`lead`, `second`, `third`) so small/distant heads (`< 150 px`) zoom toward `h3_config.ZOOM_HEAD_TARGET` (`300 px`) up to `4.0x` rather than hitting the legacy `2.0x` ceiling (as happened on `lead_fp_4227c` at `1.44x` and `lead_fp_5085b` at `2.0x`).
   - Check the planned `zoom` factor and `render_box` in `job.plan.json` (and `runs/<load>/region.npz`): if `zoom` is low (`< 2.0x` on a small head) because two visible islands separated by a foreground wipe/occlusion widen the union part box across the frame (or because a stray `Face_Neck` fragment on an edge frame widens the union box, as on `4227–4271`), **split the load across the occlusion wipe into separate sub-spans** (like `lead_fp_4227e` `4227–4257` + `lead_fp_4243b` `4243–4271`, which raised DiT zoom from `1.44x` to `2.29x` / `3.20x` and `<Video 1>` cranium enlargement from `2.133x` to `2.909x` / `3.556x`) and `--drop` any stray edge fragment.
4. **Generated `.render.txt` prompt (via `bench/what_h3_got.py`)**:
   - Generated by `bench/load_text.py` from the current `.head_reference.json` and `data/<video>/plan.json` (never carried from an older load).
   - Matches the subject's `open_frames` state (mouth transfer clauses present if and only if `open_frames` is non-empty).
5. **Recipe & Mask-Step Telemetry (`bench/flags_before_render.py` + `bench/inspect_mask_steps.py`)**:
   - `bench/flags_before_render.py` `[0]` recipe gate passes with zero mismatches against `data/<video>/plan.json`.
   - `bench/inspect_mask_steps.py --capture data/<day>_<load> --problems-only` confirms `[0]` fresh upstream provenance (on the newest Pass-1/Pass-2 delivery cut for multi-subject shots per [`docs/masked_v2v/frames_rules.md`](frames_rules.md) §0.2 Rule L6) and zero unaddressed `[1]`–`[6]` step anomalies.
6. **End-to-End `bench/load_chain.py` Execution**:
   - Never run `bench/head_reference.py`, `bench/load_text.py`, or `bench/capture_masked_run.py` standalone to patch an existing job folder in place, and never hand-edit `.chain_state.json`.
