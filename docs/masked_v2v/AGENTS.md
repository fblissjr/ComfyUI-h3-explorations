## The owner's rules for passes, prep and judging (2026-10-21)

The owner's words of 2026-10-21, each with its reasons in [`docs/wiki/decisions.md`](../wiki/decisions.md) on that date. Where a tool does not enforce a rule yet, the rule still holds and the session follows it by hand; the build that enforces it is named in `decisions.md`.

**Passes and the chain**
- **Every replacement is a pass.** A replacement is anything H3 makes in a hole: a person's face, her hair, an object such as a table. H3 makes one replacement a render, so on a shot with several, each pass is made on the newest whole-video cut that holds every other replacement laid there. Each replacement adds a link to that chain. No tool names a subject in this rule: it reads the labels in `shots.json`, `plan.json` and `laid.jsonl`.
- **A kept piece stays** unless something broken changes the composite, which is the owner's look to say (§1).
- **A re-render is not a pass.** Redoing a replacement that is alone on its shot, or the last pass with nothing laid on top of it, is one fresh preview against its source and one render. Redoing pass N of a chain breaks every pass made on its output, and those are made again, in order, on the new chain. Which cut a piece was made on is read from its render graph's source (`bench/what_h3_got.py`); no tool records the chain or flags a stale one yet. A stale kept piece is shown to the owner, not remade by rule.
- **A shot is done when every subject who can be swapped there is kept.** A subject with nothing to replace is recorded `not seen` with [`bench/shots_look.py`](../../bench/shots_look.py), on the owner's word or a look, and owes no load (§1).

**Prep**
- **Prep is not reused between loads, at any stage of the chain** (§8). Each load makes its own preview, strangers file, job, reference, text and capture; only the owner's own words carry (the cast boxes and `plan.json`). A fresh preview costs little card time next to a render made on stale prep.
- **Any render with a dependency gets its whole prep made fresh** on today's chain.
- **The one exception**, a one-variable test of a single replacement with nothing else laid on its shot, may keep its own preview, with a written reason in the job. It holds only where no code that feeds that stage has changed since it ran (masks, the part and pose models, SAM tracking, the drawing), and the window and its start and end frames have not moved. When in doubt, regenerate.
- **Moving a render's true start or end frame invalidates its prep**, unless the prep was made upfront for the whole video and the render is a single pass.

**Windows**
- **A render window is only a length on both clocks** (`h3_rules.on_both_clocks`). Where a shot is shorter than its window, the extra frames go after it (before it only at the clip's end; the owner, 2026-10-22, "just add buffer frames after", §6). By the owner's ruling of 2026-10-25 ([`docs/research/masking/2026-10-25_context_frames_and_identity_on_2050.md`](../research/masking/2026-10-25_context_frames_and_identity_on_2050.md)), the original subject is never shown unmasked in a window: where the shot's own footage continues past the piece, the load runs to the window's length masked end to end and the extra frames are chopped off at the lay; across a cut, the padding frames are held copies of the shot's own first or last frame with its region open, never the other shot's frames (2026-10-22, the owner's "seam problem"; §6), unless its edge frame's region is empty or under `video_mask.HELD_EDGE_LEAST` of the shot's middle region (`held_fell_back`, §6). This reverses the 2026-10-21 "as filmed" unmasked context default and the "best practice, not a rule" of [`docs/h3_resolutions.md`](../h3_resolutions.md), "Whole on the audio clock".

**Judging a render**
- **A render is measured against the change being made, per subject.** A part the plan replaces for that subject (`plan.json` `subjects.<s>.parts`) is compared to the subject's reference still, never to the source: dark hair that matches the original is a fault for a subject who replaces her hair, and right for one who replaces only the face. A part not replaced is compared to the source.
- **Tools show what they see:** the raw figures and frames, with what they were measured on and what they cannot see, and no rule's verdict. A tool may stop before a step that cannot be undone. A look or override the tool requires is signed by the lead on the owner's word, and its note says "not inspected". No session looks at a render before the owner does; a plan strip may be sanity-checked (decisions.md, 2026-10-22).
- **A look at one shot assembles and stacks that span alone**, or uses the load's own stacked clip where nobody else is laid on the shot. The whole-video rebuild runs only when the cut and pages must move, once a batch ([`PROTOCOL.md`](PROTOCOL.md) 5.3).

## The rules that bite when missed

### 1. What `--source` to pass in, and which subject is made first on a shot of two

- [`bench/shot_to_load.py`](../../bench/shot_to_load.py) `preview` (`bench/shot_to_load.py::step_preview`) requires `--source VIDEO`, a path, with **no silent default**. Never assume `data/<video>/shots.json`'s original raw video is the right source for the pass in hand.
- **A kept subject stays** (the owner, 2026-10-21; [`docs/wiki/decisions.md`](../wiki/decisions.md)). A subject already kept on the shot is not remade to make the other, unless something broken changes the composite, which is the owner's look to say. The assembler's `two_pieces_change_the_same_pixels` flag ([`bench/assemble_delivery.py`](../../bench/assemble_delivery.py)) names the frames where two pieces change the same pixels: a place to look, not proof that the composite is broken. No subject goes first by rule: the kept one is pass 1.
- **A single-subject shot, or pass 1 of a shot of two**: pass the video's working copy, the file `data/<video>/manifest.json` registers (`bench/shot_to_load.py::working_copy` maps a record's copy to it).
- **Pass 2, where the other subject has something to replace**: a subject with nothing to replace is recorded as `not seen` with [`bench/shots_look.py`](../../bench/shots_look.py) (`bench/shot_coverage.py::nothing_to_make` reads that line, so no load is owed). Otherwise Pass 2 is made on the newest whole-video cut (`bench/rebuild_delivery.py --video <video> --clip`, registered per [`PROTOCOL.md`](PROTOCOL.md) §1.1) that holds the kept subject, starting from a fresh `bench/shot_to_load.py preview --source <that cut> --for LABEL`: no track, mask, keep-out, reference, or text is reused from a preview of the original video (only `cast.jsonl` and `plan.json` carry). `bench/shot_to_load.py::check_pass2_source` and `check_job_pass2_source` refuse the raw source where another replacement is laid (`--allow-raw-source WHY`), refuse a stale cut older than another subject's laid row, and refuse a source cut that already holds `--for`'s own subject on those frames (for Kitchen `604-1009` and `third_fp_4647e` → `second_hfp_4647g` forensics, see [`2026-10-25_pass_contamination_and_mask_step_forensics.md`](2026-10-25_pass_contamination_and_mask_step_forensics.md) §0.1–§0.4 and [`verifying_raw_frames_and_keeps.md`](verifying_raw_frames_and_keeps.md) §2, §7–§8).
- **When Pass 1's regenerating region (`tokens`) overlaps Pass 2's subject**: [`bench/assemble_delivery.py`](../../bench/assemble_delivery.py) (`Matte.at`, `rule: "matte"`) lays a piece by the node's saved per-frame `matte` (`*_window_*_region.npz["matte"]`, which is `0` on any frame whose own mask is empty). Audit Pass-1 → Pass-2 provenance before rendering with [`bench/inspect_mask_steps.py`](../../bench/inspect_mask_steps.py) `--capture data/<day>_<load>` (§4, §11).

### 2. Bidirectional Temporal Attention & Unmasked Context Leaks (The Beard Rule)

- H3's DiT uses full bidirectional temporal attention across the entire window (`W` frames, `5n + 2` latent steps for `17n + 5` frames). Even 3–4 frames where an original subject's salient feature (such as a gray beard or eyeglasses) is left unmasked in the frozen context will propagate that feature onto the regenerated subject across the entire window.
- **Four ways unmasked features leak into the window (and what guards each)**:
  1. **Transient Sapiens class-map dips (`<= 6` frames) during fast motion**: bridged automatically by `bench/head_tube_parts.py::bridge_short_tube_dips` (`SHORT_DIP_MAX_FRAMES = 6`, `SHORT_DIP_RATIO = 0.6`).
  2. **Dropping interior frames where the face is still visible**: guarded by `bench/capture_masked_run.py::dropped_or_unmasked_face_in_context` blocking flag `dropped_frame_has_visible_face` (`DROPPED_FACE_MIN_PX = 4000`). **Never `--drop` a frame where the subject's face is visible.** On back/side turns, pass `--no-face-on` to `shot_to_load.py job` (`bench/capture_masked_run.py::grade_holds` drops only face classes from a hair+face part while keeping hair). Where the face stays in the hole (`parts_held` open) and only `<Video 1>`'s 2D marks or 3D grey head is wrong, use `--no-draw-face-on FIRST-LAST=WHY` or `--no-head-on FIRST-LAST=WHY [--no-head-form hold|blank]` (`bench/head_reference.py`).
  3. **Context frames of a short load** (2026-10-25 ruling, [`docs/research/masking/2026-10-25_context_frames_and_identity_on_2050.md`](../research/masking/2026-10-25_context_frames_and_identity_on_2050.md)): the original subject is never shown unmasked in a window. Where the shot's footage continues past the load, run to the legal window length `W` masked end to end and chop the extra frames at the lay; across a hard cut, held copies of the edge frame (`video_mask.hold_context`) with its mask open stay (`context: "face covered"` via `video_mask.pad_context` is a static mask stamp that leaves `41–59%` of a moving face uncovered and must not be used on moving footage).
  4. **Facial hair under the jaw on a face-only part** (2026-10-23): SAM 3.1's `head_track` ends at the jaw. For a face-only subject whose source shows facial hair (`plan.json` `source_facial_hair: true` with `source_facial_hair_by`), `job` writes `facial_hair: keep` and `bench/head_tube_parts.py::face_piece_kept_whole` restores the part piece joined to the face inside his person track and `FACE_COLUMN_PAD_PX` (`head_tube__<by>.json` `source_facial_hair`; see `FACIAL_HAIR_CANNOT_SEE` for clothing/Hair limits).

### 3. Always pass `--cast data/<video>/cast/cast.jsonl` and verify it exists

- [`bench/shot_to_load.py`](../../bench/shot_to_load.py) `preview` and `job` refuse missing `--cast` files cleanly (`bench/shot_to_load.py::Refused`). `preview` defaults `--cast` to `data/<video>/cast/cast.jsonl` when omitted (`bench/shot_to_load.py::step_preview`). Without the cast file, the builder cannot seed SAM 3.1 from confirmed identifications or auto-identify `lead` vs `second` vs `third` from confirmed reference embeddings.
- **What counts as a confirmed identification in `cast.jsonl` vs. Group 2 owner visual cues (the owner, 2026-10-26; [`docs/wiki/decisions.md`](../wiki/decisions.md))**:
  1. **Group 1 (`cast.jsonl` lines `1–174`)**: `who-is-who` clicks made by the owner on the HTML page — **valid and stand**.
  2. **Group 2 (owner visual cues: annotated screenshots, rough sketches, and confirmed words saying a person or segment such as `hair + face` or `hair` is visible on a frame, once read back and confirmed by the session)**: always recorded as ground truth (`plan.json` `head_marks`, shot records, `owner_sheets/`) so the owner's eyes and word are never discarded, and used to drive tracker improvements, SAM 3.1 prompting/labeling, part/segment rules, and Group 3 verification (preferring general pipeline fixes over manual frame rules). The rough hand-drawn rectangles from the 2026-10-23 screenshots that were copied directly into `cast.jsonl` (`lines 175–192, 194–196`) are **never included directly as raw box seeds**.
  3. **Group 3 (`cast.jsonl` lines `197–209`)**: exact SAM person-detection boxes verified by a session on the SAM detection masks using the owner's Group 2 visual cues — **valid and stand**.

### 4. Pre-render & Post-capture Telemetry (`facts_pass.py`, `inspect_mask_steps.py`, `flags_before_render.py`, `step_carves.json`, `code_stamp.py`)

- Run [`bench/facts_pass.py`](../../bench/facts_pass.py) on the capture directory to inspect `face_outside_region_px` (catches chin/cheek cutouts where `(s_face | (head_track & ~s_hair))` falls outside `region`), `other_overlap_px`, and `mouth_mode` before spending GPU render minutes.
- Run [`bench/inspect_mask_steps.py`](../../bench/inspect_mask_steps.py) `--capture data/<day>_<load> --problems-only --strips <out.jpg>` before queueing a render (§11). It audits eight failure modes in ~5 seconds on CPU: `[0]` input & pass-dependency provenance (`laid.jsonl` pass number, source consistency across `preview`, `strangers`, and `manifest.json`, stale inputs older than producer code git commits, and Pass-1 blue-region overlap and SAM track collapse on Pass-2+ loads), `[1]` held-padding bleed into kept shot steps, `[2]` blue-only frames (`0 px` red mask inside an active blue latent step), `[3]` tiny red speck frames inside a blue latent step (`< 25%` of median), `[4]` edge-shrunk whole-head frames (`< 75%` of immediate neighbor), `[5]` `strangers` self-swallowing the subject's own emerging head, `[6]` SAM 3.1 `people.npz` strangers missed by whole-frame Sapiens2 inside the blue region, and `[7]` per-frame gap recovery and visual red/blue/contour crop strips (`--strips`).
- Run [`bench/flags_before_render.py`](../../bench/flags_before_render.py) `data/<day>_<load> --load <load> --initial <session>` to print and sign off the five diagnostic blocks (`[0]` recipe gate against `plan.json` `recipe` and `shot_overrides`, `[1]` `flags.json`, `[2]` `marks_against_the_body_model` + `face_on_the_track` + `mouthless_reference`, `[3]` `runs/<run>/step_carves.json`, and `[4]` held/emptied frames); `bench/load_chain.py::the_flags_read` refuses `render` unless `<capture>/looks/<load>.flags_read.json` is recorded for the current `flags.json`.
- Inspect `runs/<run>/step_carves.json` (`bench/capture_masked_run.py::step_keepout_carves`) for latent-step keep-out cell carves touching the subject's head track or carried part.
- All derived `.npz` / `.json` artifacts carry `writer_stamp` (`bench/_lib/code_stamp.py::writer_stamp`, `bench/_lib/code_stamp.py::npz_writer_fields`). `bench/_lib/code_stamp.py::check_upstream_mtime` compares file times and returns warnings: [`bench/draw_face_points.py`](../../bench/draw_face_points.py) prints them, and [`bench/head_reference.py`](../../bench/head_reference.py) and [`bench/measure_moge2_depth.py`](../../bench/measure_moge2_depth.py) add them to their refusal to write over an output that is there. Nothing in `load_chain.py` refuses a stale input on its own (§8); run [`bench/inspect_mask_steps.py`](../../bench/inspect_mask_steps.py) to compare every input artifact's mtime against its producer code's latest git commit.

### 6. 24.0 fps End-to-End, One Shot = One Render (`<= 345` Frames), Lengths on Both Clocks, and What Context/Overlap Frames Are Thrown Away

- **Exact `24.0` fps (`24/1`) everywhere**: Every working copy, preview, reference, render, and assembled multi-pass delivery cut ([`bench/rebuild_delivery.py`](../../bench/rebuild_delivery.py), [`bench/assemble_delivery.py`](../../bench/assemble_delivery.py)) is forced to `24.0` fps (`bench/shot_to_load.py::working_copy` and [`bench/masked_job.py`](../../bench/masked_job.py) refuse any non-24 fps source).
- **One Shot = One Prompt = One Render (Never Split a Shot `<= 345` Frames)**:
  - Every shot of `<= 345` frames (`max(h3_rules.chain_lengths())`) renders as **one single-window H3 load** (`workflows/masked_job_graph.py::window_for`) on both clocks (`h3_rules.on_both_clocks`: `39, 90, 141, 192, 243, 294, 345`; [`frames_rules.md`](frames_rules.md)) with zero mid-shot seams.
  - **Interior occlusions stay inside the single whole-shot load**: never split a shot before/after an occlusion. Account for occluded frames with `capture_masked_run.py verify --no-mask`, leaving them as the source (`left_as_the_source_with_no_mask`, `bench/capture_masked_run.py::verify_checks`) only where she is truly hidden and marked `hidden` in `plan.json` `head_marks` (§10).
  - **Multi-window chained shots (`N > 345` frames, e.g. Kitchen `604–1009` and Guitar Solo `2050–2622`)**: still run as **one load** (`shot_to_load.py job [--seam ...]`), chained across windows on `h3_rules.chain_lengths` (`141, 192, 243, 294, 345`) with `context_frames = 90` (`workflows/h3_config.py::MASKED_JOB_CONTEXT`) overlap frames that the stitcher discards after decoding.
- **How Preprocessed Inputs Lock to `[shot.first .. shot.last]`**:
  1. `shot_to_load.py preview --shot <first>` writes `track`, `head_track`, `parts`, `classes`, `pose`, and `mesh` starting at frame `0 == shot.first` for `N` frames (`strangers` runs once per preview at the `job` step via `bench/strangers.py`).
  2. `shot_to_load.py reference` writes `<Video 1>` (`<subject>_head_reference_<first>_<last>.mp4`) starting at `0 == shot.first` for `N` frames.
  3. `shot_to_load.py text` writes `"frames": [shot.first, shot.last]` in the job JSON.
  4. `capture_masked_run.py verify` enforces `at_is_the_previews_first_frame` (`at == shot.first`), `inputs_reach_the_span`, and `inputs_at_the_canvas` (`1024x768` at `24.0` fps).
- **Window Padding (`N < W`) & the 2026-10-25 Three-Path Context Rule** ([`docs/research/masking/2026-10-25_context_frames_and_identity_on_2050.md`](../research/masking/2026-10-25_context_frames_and_identity_on_2050.md); [`frames_rules.md`](frames_rules.md)):
  - When `N < W`, padding goes after `[first .. last]` (`before = 0`, `after = W - N` in `workflows/masked_job_graph.py::window_pad`; `before > 0` only at the source clip's end because `comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py` encodes in 17-frame clips).
  - **The original subject is never shown unmasked in a window**:
    1. **Where footage continues past the piece**: run the load to the legal window length `W` masked end to end (`pad_after == 0`), and chop the extra denoised frames at the lay via `laid.jsonl`'s `frames` field (`bench/laid.py`). (Never rely on `as filmed` or `face covered` in `video_mask.py::pad_context` when the subject moves in padding frames.)
    2. **Across a hard cut into another shot**: `workflows/masked_job_graph.py::held_context` holds copies of the shot's edge frame (`video_mask.hold_context`) with its masks open and silences the audio track (`audio_freeze_song.silenced`), unless the edge frame's region is empty or `< video_mask.HELD_EDGE_LEAST` (`0.25`) of the shot's middle region (`held_fell_back`). After VAE decode, `audio_freeze_song.py::MiniMaxH3AudioFreezeSong.execute` discards `pad_before` and `pad_after`.
- **No stale pose or mask data**: never reuse masks, SAM tracks, Sapiens2 part or class maps, or pose files across a change to what made them (§8; [`chain_trace_and_remakes.md`](chain_trace_and_remakes.md)).

### 7. Head Reference Mouth Drawing & Unified `<Subject 2>` Prompt (Closed = 3D Gray Mesh Lips; Open = Black Interior + White Outline)

> **Full specification:** [`reference_and_prompt_rules.md`](reference_and_prompt_rules.md) holds the complete rules, constants, and history for mouth forms (`open_mouth`, `closed_mouth`, `MOUTH_BEND`), eye forms (`DOWNCAST_RING_OUTSIDE_MIN`, `EYE_OPEN`, `EYES_SHUT_FORMS`), reference crops (`head`, `face`, `cranium`), and the unified `<Subject 2>` prompt (`bench/load_text.py`).

- **No mouth smoothing (`smooth_mouth_area` retired, 2026-10-20)**; **closed-mouth frames stay as natural 3D gray mesh lips (`mouths[f] = None`)** unless `reference.closed_mouth: "outlined"` (`anchored_closed_mouth` + `MOUTH_BEND`); **open-mouth frames draw black interior + white outline** (`reference.open_mouth`: default `inner outline`, or `outlined` on Sapiens2 class-map lips via `anchored_mouth` and `fit_mouth_to_head`).
- **A lowered eye is drawn with a dark iris (`DOWNCAST_RING_OUTSIDE_MIN`), and a closed eye is painted over** (`--eyes-shut-on ... --eyes-shut-form closed|lowered|lids|none`; lids opened downward to `EYE_OPEN` so a piece of the black iris shows).
- **Reference crop (`reference.crop`, `bench/head_reference.py::CROP_FORMS`)**: `head` (default), `face`, or `cranium` (frames the body model's bald grey head above mid-face via `bench/crop_reference_to_head.py::grey_head`; every subject of `fun` takes `cranium`).
- **Unified `<Subject 2>` prompt (`bench/load_text.py`)**: held byte for byte to the owner's 2026-10-22 form by `bench/check_load_text.py`. Names `text.picture_hair` and `text.hair_words` (never eye, eyebrow, or skin words), adds `MOUTH_CLAUSE` only when `mouth_drawn_open` is true, and uses `TRANSFERRED_DRAWN_NO_MOUTH` / `FOLLOWS_DRAWN_NO_MOUTH` when `mouth_drawn` is false on every frame.

### 8. What a change forces: the chain in trace order

> **Full stage-by-stage trace (Stages 0–7) & remake rules:** [`chain_trace_and_remakes.md`](chain_trace_and_remakes.md) §1 holds the complete stage inputs, outputs, span words, and remake rules.

- **Default remake**: a fresh preview (`preview --remake`) and every stage after it (`decisions.md`, 2026-10-21). Reusing an existing preview is allowed only when testing a single downstream change and `git log <preview_commit>..HEAD` shows zero changes to Stage 1 producer code.
- **No remake starts later than `job --remake`, and none starts at `first`**: `first`, `plan`, and `render` each run once under a load's name (`bench/load_chain.py`).
- **Audit with `bench/inspect_mask_steps.py`**: `load_chain.py` does not compare producer code commits against input file mtimes (`inputs_written_together` only checks sibling file timestamps inside a preview). Always run `bench/inspect_mask_steps.py --capture data/<day>_<load>` (§11) to verify input provenance against git commits.

### 11. End-to-end mask-step & provenance forensics (`bench/inspect_mask_steps.py`, 2026-10-25)

- **When to run it**: after `load_chain.py on-plan` (before every render) and immediately when debugging any failed or suspect render ([`docs/masked_v2v/2026-10-25_pass_contamination_and_mask_step_forensics.md`](2026-10-25_pass_contamination_and_mask_step_forensics.md)):
  ```bash
  CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES= H3_INPUT_DIR=<share>/input H3_OUTPUT_DIR=<share>/output \
    <comfy-venv>/bin/python bench/inspect_mask_steps.py \
    --capture data/<day>_<load> --problems-only --strips /tmp/<load>_strips.jpg
  ```
- **What it checks (`[0]`–`[7]`)**:
  - `[0]` **Input & pass-dependency provenance**: Pass order vs. `laid.jsonl`, creation (`%w`) + modification (`%y`) dates, `stale_at_build` (inputs already older than producer-code commits when the capture was built) vs. `code_updated_since_capture`, `strangers` presence, `depth.npz` (`bench/measure_moge2_depth.py`) usage vs. unsettled `contested_px`, and prior-pass DiT blue-region overlap and SAM 3.1 track collapse between raw and Pass-2 previews.
  - `[1]` **Held-padding bleed into kept shot steps**: maps `(1, 4, 4, 4, 4)` latent steps (`video_mask.FRAME_PER_TOKEN`, `HELD_EDGE_EMPTY_RUN = 4`) and flags any boundary step where `PAD_*` frames carry `> 0 px` alongside `0 px` in-shot frames.
  - `[2]` **Blue-only frames**: in-shot frames with `carried_px == 0` inside an active blue latent step (left unlaid by `video_mask.unlaid_frames` while the DiT regenerates the 4-frame step).
  - `[3]` **Speck-in-blue frames**: frames with `0 < carried_px < 25%` of the step's max and below the part floor inside an active blue step.
  - `[4]` **Edge-shrunk whole-head frames**: frames in `whole_head_frames` with `< 75%` of an immediate neighbor's area right before/after an occlusion gap.
  - `[5]` **`strangers` self-swallow frames**: frames where `carried < 50%` of median and `raw_strangers` swallows `>= 30%` of the nearest clean reference head.
  - `[6]` **Missed SAM 3.1 strangers inside blue region**: non-subject people in `_people.npz` (`score >= 0.70`, `>= 256 px` inside the active blue region) where `strangers.mkv` covers `< 25%`.
  - `[7]` **Gap & occlusion recovery table + `--strips <out.jpg>`**: per-frame recovery table and annotated crop strip (`RED` = `carried_px`, `BLUE` = regenerated latent step, `GREEN` = `strangers`, `YELLOW` = recoverable `clean_whole` reference).
- **What it does NOT do**: it is strictly read-only (modifies no `.npz` or `.mkv` file, runs no GPU model, and does not judge facial likeness in a finished render).
