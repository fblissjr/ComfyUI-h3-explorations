# Frame rules: how a masked load's frames, latent steps, zooms, and face drawings line up with what H3 sees

Last updated: 2026-10-31.

One page for the frame, latent-step, zoom, and `<Video 1>` face-drawing rules of a masked load: how H3's three temporal scopes (`4k + 1` latent steps, `17`-frame causal VAE clips, and `W`-frame DiT windows) interact, how long a window is (`>= 39` frames), why splitting a `< 39`-frame shot fails, how the video VAE and Qwen3-VL text encoder cut the window, how node render zoom and `<Video 1>` reference crop zoom trade off tokens vs. pixel softness, and what closed/open mouths and eyes look like on `<Video 1>`. Every figure points at the code that sets it; when this page and the code disagree, the code is right ([`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md), and the root `AGENTS.md`, "Do not trust prose"). The lane's other rules are in [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) and [`reference_and_prompt_rules.md`](reference_and_prompt_rules.md); the legal lengths and their cost for H3 in general are in [`docs/h3_resolutions.md`](../h3_resolutions.md). The full window, padding, held-edge, and post-decode slicing trace in code is in [`docs/masked_v2v/code_map_contracts.md`](code_map_contracts.md) (Section `2b`) and [`docs/research/masking/2026-10-26_code_map_and_simplification_plan.md`](../research/masking/2026-10-26_code_map_and_simplification_plan.md).

## 0. The H3 Latent Frame, Step, Clip, and Context Rules at a Glance

### 0.1 The Three Temporal Scopes (Why H3 Is Both Local to `1 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4` Steps/Clips and Bidirectional Across the Window)

H3 does not treat a `39`-to-`345` frame window as a single uniform tensor at every stage. Instead, three nested temporal scopes govern what sees what:

| Scope | Size in Pixel Frames | Code & Exact Mechanism | What Is Coupled / Bidirectional Here | Failure Mode When Violated |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Latent Step (`4k + 1`: `1` frame on clip frame `0`, `4` frames on steps `1..4`)** | Frame `0` of each 17-frame VAE clip = **1 frame**; Steps `1..4` of each 17-frame clip = **4 frames each** (`video_mask.py::run_lengths`: `[1, 4, 4, 4, 4]` per 17-frame clip; `12` steps for `39` frames) | `video_mask.py::token_mask` (`video_mask.py:131-186`) max-pools pixel masks over `16x16` spatial cells (`CELL = 16`) and across all `1` or `4` frames of the latent step (`amax(dim=(1, 3, 5))`). Keep-out carving (`tokens * (1 - theirs * (1 - own))`) and `KSamplerX0Inpaint` source-latent replacement (`out * denoise_mask + latent_image * (1 - denoise_mask)`) operate **per latent step**. `comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py::MiniMaxH3Model` (`comfy/model_base.py:312-331`) max-pools `2x2` cells (`32x32` px) per DiT token. | **All 4 frames of a step share one `denoise_mask` token and one latent token `z`.** Neither the mask nor the inpaint blend can vary from frame to frame inside a 4-frame step. | **(a) 1-frame keep-out carves all 4 frames**: If `strangers` or `others` (`theirs`) touches a margin cell (`own == 0`) on *any 1 frame* of a 4-frame step, that cell is carved (`denoise_mask = 0`, source latent `z` locked in) for **all 4 frames** of the step.<br>**(b) `BLUE-ONLY` (`0 px` mask inside an active 4-frame step)**: On `second_hfp_4912r`, a `316 px` sliver on frame `4921` activated Step 3 (`4921–4924`) while `4922–4924` had `0 px` mask, and an `815 px` sliver on `4926` activated Step 4 (`4925–4928`) while `4925` had `0 px` mask—packing the foreground occluder's dark hair directly into the shared 4-frame token. |
| **2. Video VAE Clip (`17` frames = `1 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4` = `5` steps)** | **Encoder**: `17` frames per clip (`0..16`, `17..33`, `34..38` padded with `12` copies of frame `38` on a 39-frame window; `token_drop = 3` drops those `3` tail steps to yield `5n + 2` steps).<br>**Decoder**: overlapping chunks of `5 + 2 = 7` latent steps (`28` frames). | `comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py::encode_temporal` (`comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py:755-786`) slices the video into **independent 17-frame clips** (`clip_length = 17`) before calling `EncoderFCN3D`. Inside each 17-frame clip, `comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py::CausalConv3d` (`comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py:72-104`) zero-pads only the temporal front (`(0, 0, 0, 0, 2*p, 0)`).<br>`comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py::decode_temporal` (`comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py:788-824`) runs `ViT3DDecoder` in chunks of `tokens_chunk_size + token_overlap = 5 + 2 = 7` latent steps. | **The 3D VAE encoder never sees across a 17-frame clip boundary (`16 -> 17` or `33 -> 34`), and inside a 17-frame clip it is strictly causal** (frame `t` only sees earlier frames `<= t` within that 17-frame clip). **The ViT VAE decoder is bidirectional only within its local `7`-step (`28`-frame) chunk**, never across a `100+` frame window. | **(a) Clip 3 standalone frame `34` (`Step 10`) pin**: In a 39-frame window, Clip 3 starts at frame index `34` (`Step 10`, 1-frame standalone step). When a 35-frame shot (`0..34`) holds frame `34` across `35..38` (`Step 11`) plus `12` VAE tail copies, **all 17 frames of Clip 3 are copies of frame `34`**. On `second_hfp_4912e` (`45.8%` unmasked crown) and `4912r` (`strangers` `c == 0` swallowing the left crown on `4946`), frame `34` (`4946`) pinned dark brown hair across all 17 frames of Clip 3.<br>**(b) Outnumbered boundary frame in Clip 3**: On `second_hfp_4479e`, frame `4509` sat alone at frame `34` (Clip 3 start) with 4 frames of the next shot + 12 tail copies, flashing a bright square over her head. |
| **3. DiT Sampling Window (`W = 17n + 5` on both clocks)** | `39, 90, 141, 192, 243, 294, 345` frames (`12, 27, 42, 57, 72, 87, 102` latent steps) (`h3_rules.py::on_both_clocks`, `workflows/masked_job_graph.py::window_for`) | `comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py::DiTBlock` (`comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:279-299`) runs full 3D self-attention across all `5n + 2` latent steps of the active window `W` (masked V2V graphs are in `bench/check_attention_defaults.py::SOL_EXEMPT_STEMS`, so dense self-attention is used). ### 0.2 The Six Operative Latent-Step, Context & Multi-Subject Pass Rules

1. **Rule L1 — Minimum window is `39` frames (`12` latent steps), and a `< 39`-frame shot must never be split into a shorter sub-shot (`second_hfp_4927b`)**:
   - The shortest length on both the video (`17n + 5`) and audio (`24 fps -> 40 Hz`) clocks is **`39` frames** (`h3_rules.py::on_both_clocks`: `39, 90, 141, 192, 243, 294, 345`).
   - When a job span `[first .. last]` has `N < 39` frames, `workflows/masked_job_graph.py::window_pad` (`workflows/masked_job_graph.py:600-608`) sets `before = 0, after = 39 - N`, extending the loaded source span to `[first .. last + after]`.
   - Crucially, `workflows/masked_job_graph.py::held_context` (`workflows/masked_job_graph.py:610-624`) only freezes frames **outside `shot_span`** (`after_held = max(0, (last + after) - shot_last)`). Any frame between `last + 1` and `shot_last` is inside the same shot and is **not held**—it enters `video_mask.py::pad_context` as a live unheld source frame!
   - **Proven failure (`second_hfp_4927b`, 2026-10-29)**: Splitting the 35-frame shot `4912–4946` into a 14-frame sub-job `4927–4940` caused `window_for(14)` to pad `after = 25` (`4941–4965`). `held_context` held only the `19` post-cut frames (`4947–4965`), leaving the **6 in-shot frames `4941–4946` as unheld, unmasked source frames** where the original dark-haired actress was visible (`4943–4946`), while `bench/head_reference.py` also refused `4927` because starting a load on the first frame after an occlusion leaves an unanchored edge track (`THE FACE IS OFF THE SUBJECT'S TRACK ... at the span's edge`).
   - **What to do instead**: Keep a `< 39`-frame shot as **one single-window load** (`4912–4946`, 35 frames padded with 4 held post-cut frames `4947–4950`), and handle interior occlusions inside that single load via `head_marks` (`hidden`), `--no-head-on`, `--no-face-on`, and `--drop`. On shots `<= 345` frames where wide lateral travel or an interior occlusion wipe holds `zoom_box` (`set_by = "the box"`) or `cranium_box` down (e.g. `516–599` or `4227–4271`), splitting into sub-loads is allowed **only** when the `second_hfp_4927b` guard holds: no split load may leave visible in-shot frames of the same shot unheld and unmasked inside its `W`-frame window ([`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) §6).
2. **Rule L2 — Eliminate `BLUE-ONLY` frames (`0 px` mask inside an active 4-frame latent step) and boundary slivers (`second_hfp_4912r` vs. `4912t`)**:
   - In a 4-frame latent step (`1..4`, `5..8`, `9..12`, `13..16` of each 17-frame clip), if even 1 frame has a non-empty mask, `video_mask.py::token_mask` activates the step (`denoise_mask > 0`) for all 4 frames, while `video_mask.py::unlaid_frames` (`video_mask.py:349-388`) zeroes the output `matte` on any individual frame whose pixel mask is `0 px` (`BLUE-ONLY`).
   - Worse, inside the 3D causal VAE encoder and DiT, the 1–3 unmasked (`0 px`) frames of that active 4-frame step feed the foreground occluder's pixels directly into the shared 4-frame latent token (`bench/inspect_mask_steps.py` Check `[2]` and `[3]`).
   - **Fix**: When a subject enters or exits full occlusion inside a shot, drop or zero isolated 1-frame boundary slivers (`--drop`, `--no-head-on`, or `erase_frames`) so the fully occluded 4-frame step is `[0, 0, 0, 0]` (`0` active tokens) and the re-emergence step starts cleanly (`second_hfp_4912t`, where Step 3 `4921–4924` became `0` tokens and Step 4 `4925–4928` started at `4927`).
3. **Rule L3 — Guard Clip 3's standalone frame `34` (`Step 10` of a 39-frame window)**:
   - In a 39-frame window, `Step 10` is **frame index `34` alone** (the 1-frame initial token of Clip 3, frames `34..38`). When a 35-frame shot (`0..34`) ends at a camera cut after frame `34`, `video_mask.py::hold_context` copies frame `34` into `35..38` (`Step 11`) and `comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py::encode_temporal` copies frame `38` `12` more times—so **frame `34` is repeated 17 times to fill all of Clip 3**.
   - If frame `34` has an empty/tiny mask (`< video_mask.HELD_EDGE_LEAST = 0.25` of median), `video_mask.py::held_edge_source` steps inward to hold the last clean frame. If frame `34` has a partial mask where the crown or cheek is unmasked (as on `second_hfp_4912r`, where `strangers` swallowed the left crown on `4946`), that unmasked original hair is pinned into Step 10 and all 16 held copies of Clip 3. Always inspect frame `34` (`last` of a 35-frame shot) in `frame_table.md` (`hair_outside_hole_share`) and `step_carves.json`.
4. **Rule L4 — Keep all attached hair inside `own`, and strip `c == 0` (`Background`) `strangers` spill from the hair margin (`16ccd81a`, `ba86fbe9`)**:
   - **Why keep-out margin carves leak dark hair**: In `video_mask.py::token_mask` (`video_mask.py:165-171`), `own` is the un-dilated core mask (`parts_held`), `tokens` is the dilated mask (`parts_held` + `margin`), and `theirs` (`others` / `strangers`) carves any `16x16` cell in the `margin` ring (`own == 0`) back to `0` (`tokens * (1 - theirs * (1 - own))`).
   - **Attached hair within the head-track band (`16ccd81a`)**: `bench/head_tube_parts.py::head_tube_for_capture` expands `head_track_bool` into connected Sapiens Hair (`class 4`) within the head-track vertical band `[hy0, hy1]` (and upward into the crown when `>= 256 px` sits above `hy0`), and `bench/frame_table.py` reports `hair_outside_hole_share` (`hair_outside_hole_px / hair_px`) on every frame so unmasked back-and-sides hair is never left in the margin.
   - **Stripping Sapiens `c == 0` (`Background`) SAM spill from `strangers` (`ba86fbe9`)**: SAM 3.1's unowned person masks (`strangers`) spill 5–15 px across foreground occlusion boundaries into Sapiens `class 0` (`Background`) halo pixels over the subject's hair. `bench/capture_masked_run.py::_files` strips `whole_classes == 0` pixels from `strangers.mkv` inside a Hair subject's dilated hole (`margin_px + CELL`) on active/`hers` frames, and `bench/capture_masked_run.py::her_hair_in_hole` (`crown_from_head`) recovers top-third crown pixels through `strangers` where `whole_classes == 0`. On `second_hfp_4912t`, this cut `strangers` latent-step carves from `2,986` to `1,613` cells (`+148` uncarved `final_units`, `332 -> 480`, from the recovered hair crown on `4946`, while `196` carved cells remain on the white hat) and raised rendered hair from dark brown (`med L* = 11.4, b* = +16.0` on `4912r`) to golden-blonde (`med L* = 25.5, p90 = 58.8, b* = +30.0`).
5. **Rule L5 — Never show the original subject unmasked anywhere in the window (The Three Context Paths, 2026-10-25)**:
   - Every frame of the shot sits in a window with its own shot first (`before = 0`, except at the very end of the source video), and **the original subject is never shown unmasked in a window** ([`docs/research/masking/2026-10-25_context_frames_and_identity_on_2050.md`](../research/masking/2026-10-25_context_frames_and_identity_on_2050.md)):
     - **Path 1 (Subject absent in context frames)**: If the subject has genuinely exited the frame on the padding frames, real unmasked context frames are safe.
     - **Path 2 (Shot continues past the piece with the subject present)**: Run the load to the legal window length `W` (`39, 90, 141, 192, 243, 294, 345`) **masked and `<Video 1>`-conditioned end to end** (`pad_after == 0`), and chop the extra rendered frames off at the lay via `laid.jsonl`'s `frames` field (`bench/laid.py`). Never rely on `context: "as filmed"` or `context: "face covered"` (`video_mask.py::pad_context` stamps a static edge mask that misses `41–59%` of a moving face).
     - **Path 3 (Shot ends at a hard camera cut)**: `workflows/masked_job_graph.py::held_context` holds copies of the shot's last clean masked frame (`video_mask.py::hold_context`, guarded by `video_mask.HELD_EDGE_LEAST = 0.25`) with its masks open, and `audio_freeze_song.py::MiniMaxH3AudioFreezeSong.execute` discards the held padding frames after VAE decode.
6. **Rule L6 — Sequential Multi-Subject Pass Chain & Downstream DAG Invalidation (`Pass 1 -> Pass 2 -> Pass 3`, 2026-10-21 / 2026-10-31)**:
   - On a shot with more than one subject, remaking any pass, even one frame of it, means **every pass from that one onward is remade in order, as whole loads, each after the one before it is kept and laid**:
     - **Which subject is Pass 1**: Where an earlier pass is already kept and standing on the shot, it stays as Pass 1. When **every** pass on a multi-subject shot is being remade, `second` (`hair + face`, `margin = 64`/`128`, reasoned) goes first as Pass 1 on the raw working-copy source video (`fun_0000_6394_24p_sound_in_step.mov`) so downstream passes segment against her replaced blonde hair silhouette.
     - **Downstream passes (`Pass 2`, `Pass 3`)**: Each downstream pass takes its **whole data chain from a fresh preview on a rebuilt cut that holds the earlier passes and not the subject being made** (`bench/shot_to_load.py preview --source <rebuilt-cut.mp4> --for <subject> --remake --queue` -> `strangers` -> `job` with `depth.on = true` -> `reference` -> `text` -> `first` -> `plan` -> `on-plan` -> `render` -> `land`).
     - **Earlier passes stand, with one exception (`bench/assemble_delivery.py::restore_weight`)**: An earlier kept pass is not made from a later pass's output, so remaking Pass 2 or Pass 3 leaves Pass 1 standing (e.g. `third_fp_4912` on top of kept `second_hfp_4912t`)—**except** when `second` is remade in a chain where another subject went before it (as on `4647–4725`, where `third_fp_4647e` was Pass 1 on raw before `second_hfp_4647i`). In that case, remake the whole chain with `second` first, because the earlier piece's masks hold `second`'s original hair (`bench/assemble_delivery.py::restore_weight`).

## 1. The window's length

- A masked load renders in one window whose length is on both clocks: on the video's `17n + 5` grid and whole on the
  audio's (`h3_rules.py::on_both_clocks`, `h3_rules.py::FPS`, `h3_rules.py::AUDIO_LATENT_RATE`), the shortest that holds the shot
  (`workflows/masked_job_graph.py::window_for`). The lengths: **39, 90, 141, 192, 243, 294, 345**.
- A shot longer than the longest (`> 345` frames) runs as chained windows inside one load (`h3_rules.py::chain_lengths`,
  `workflows/h3_config.py::MASKED_JOB_CONTEXT`); [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) §6 has how the `90`-frame overlap is thrown away.

## 2. How the video VAE cuts the window

From `comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py` (the constructor's `clip_length`, `time_down`, `token_drop`, `encode_temporal`, and `decode_temporal`):

- The window is encoded in **clips of 17 frames, each on its own** (`comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py:755-786`). A short last clip is filled out with copies of its own last frame. Because `encode_temporal` chunks by `17` frames independently and `EncoderFCN3D` uses `comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py::CausalConv3d` (`comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py:72-104`, front-only temporal zero padding), **the VAE encoder never sees across a 17-frame clip boundary, and inside each 17-frame clip it is strictly causal** (frame `t` only sees frames `<= t` of that clip).
- Inside a 17-frame clip, the first frame (`frame 0` of the clip) is **one latent step by itself** and the other 16 go **4 to a step** (`[1, 4, 4, 4, 4]` = 5 steps a clip, mirrored in `video_mask.py::run_lengths`).
- The last 3 steps of the whole window (`12` tail-padded pixel frames) are dropped (`token_drop = 3`), which is why a `17n + 5` window has `5n + 2` steps (`12` steps for `39` frames, `27` steps for `90` frames).
- On decode (`comfy/ldm/minimax/vae.py:788-824`), `ViT3DDecoder` decodes in overlapping chunks of `tokens_chunk_size + token_overlap = 5 + 2 = 7` latent steps (`28` pixel frames), with bidirectional attention only inside each `7`-step chunk.

So in a 39-frame window:
- **Clip 1** is frames `0–16` (`Steps 0–4`: `[0]`, `[1..4]`, `[5..8]`, `[9..12]`, `[13..16]`),
- **Clip 2** is frames `17–33` (`Steps 5–9`: `[17]`, `[18..21]`, `[22..25]`, `[26..29]`, `[30..33]`),
- **Clip 3** is frames `34–38` (`Step 10` = `[34]`, `Step 11` = `[35..38]`) filled with `12` copies of frame `38` for the encoder (dropped after encoding by `token_drop = 3`).

**What goes wrong when a shot's frame is outnumbered or half-masked in Clip 3:**
- `second_hfp_4479e` (31 frames in 39, padded 4 before and 4 after): its last frame, `4509`, was window frame `34`, alone at the start of Clip 3 with the next shot's 4 frames and 12 copies of the last of them. Everything regenerated round her came out brighter on `4509` alone, a square (the owner, 2026-10-22). Historical `third_fp_4602c` (before `before = 0` and `hold_context`, queued for remake) showed no such frame: its last frame sat in a clip of 16 of its own frames and one of the next shot's (22 before and 23 after in 90, so `4646` is window frame `66`, in the clip of frames `51–67`).
- `second_hfp_4912e` and `second_hfp_4912r` (35 frames `4912–4946` in 39, padded with 4 held copies after): frame `4946` is window frame `34` (`Step 10`, standalone) and is held across `35–38` (`Step 11`) plus `12` tail copies—so all 17 frames of Clip 3 are copies of `4946`. On `4912e`, `4946` had an empty mask; on `4912r`, `strangers` `c == 0` swallowed the left half of her crown on `4946` (`hair_outside_hole_share = 40.3%`), pinning dark hair across Clip 3 until `ba86fbe9` stripped `c == 0` `strangers` from the hair margin (`4912t`).

## 3. How the text encoder and video VAE see `<Video 1>`

- Qwen3-VL is given `<Video 1>` (the face drawing) at two frames a second, every 12th frame from **window frame 0**
  (`reference_conditioning.py`, `sample_indices = range(0, frames, FPS // 2)`), and fuses each pair into one picture,
  labelled with their mean time (`comfy/text_encoders/minimax.py`, the `video` branch). So it sees **one fused picture a second** (pairs `(0, 12)`, `(24, 36)`, ...), each the blend of two frames half a second apart.
- **Avoid solid-black (`--no-head-form blank`) frames on Qwen sample indices (`0, 12, 24, ...`)**: If a frame at a multiple of 12 is blanked to `100%` black (`blank`), Qwen3-VL fuses a black canvas into that second's visual token (`second_hfp_4912o` frame `4924`, load index `12`). Use `--no-head-on FIRST-LAST=WHY` with the default `--no-head-form hold` (`bench/head_reference.py:725-755`), which freezes the last clean 3D grey head frame across the occlusion.
- The padding is part of what it samples: the drawing is held at its first and last frames over the padding
  (`video_mask.py`, the motion reference's padding). With the padding after the shot, its first picture is the shot's
  first moment, and any picture past the shot's end is a held copy of its last.
- `<Video 1>` also goes to the video model whole: every frame is VAE-encoded (`motion_vae`, set in
  `workflows/masked_job_graph.py::load`), in the same 17-frame clips as the window (§2). So the padding rule lines up
  `<Video 1>`'s clips with the window's for the video model, and its samples with the shot's start for the text encoder.
- Both see `<Video 1>` at the canvas's size (`1024x768`): the text encoder in `16 px` patches merged `2x2` to `32 px`, the video model in `32 px` tokens (§6.2).
- `<Picture 1>`, the still, goes to the VAE (at `2048` short edge) and to Qwen3-VL as one image (`qwen_view` `"shared"`,
  `workflows/h3_config.py::MASKED_JOB_STILL_VIEW`; [`docs/research/sglang_h3_pipeline.md`](../research/sglang_h3_pipeline.md), §2 and §3), so the two see it alike.

## 4. What fills the padding

- **After the shot, always** (`workflows/masked_job_graph.py::window_pad`: `before = 0`, `after = window - shot`).
  The one exception: a shot ending so near the source's last frame that the frames after it do not exist takes the
  rest before it: held copies of its first frame where those frames are another shot (the guard below applies), or
  masked real frames where the shot goes on.
- **Never show the original subject unmasked in the window** (the owner's ruling, 2026-10-25; [`docs/research/masking/2026-10-25_context_frames_and_identity_on_2050.md`](../research/masking/2026-10-25_context_frames_and_identity_on_2050.md), reversing the 2026-10-21 `as filmed` unmasked context default after `lead_fp_2050c` and `lead_fp_2050e` re-synthesised the singer's face from unmasked context frames):
  - **Where the shot's own footage continues past the piece**: the load runs to the legal window length (`39, 90, 141, 192, 243, 294, 345`) with every frame masked end to end from the preview's masks (`pad_after == 0`), and the extra frames past the piece are chopped off at the lay (`laid.jsonl` `frames` field). (Never split a `< 39`-frame shot into a sub-shot that stops before `shot_last`; see §0.2 Rule L1 and `second_hfp_4927b`.)
  - **Where the frames after the shot cross a hard cut into another shot**: **held copies of the shot's last frame, with its masks** (`workflows/masked_job_graph.py::held_context`, `video_mask.py::hold_context`; the shot's boundaries are `data/<video>/shots.json`'s).
- **The guard**: where the shot's edge frame has no mask, or less than `video_mask.py::HELD_EDGE_LEAST` (`0.25`) of the shot's median
  region, `video_mask.py::held_edge_source` steps inward to the nearest shot frame whose mask clears `HELD_EDGE_LEAST` of the median,
  holds that frame's image and masks into the padding (`video_mask.py::hold_context`, keeping all kept shot frames untouched), and
  records the step in `held_fell_back` (`"hold shot frame N"`), unless that side's outermost `video_mask.py::HELD_EDGE_EMPTY_RUN`
  (`max(FRAME_PER_TOKEN) == 4`, one full latent step) shot frames are all `0 px` while the shot has a mask elsewhere (when the
  subject has left or been hidden for at least a latent step at that edge of the shot, as on `second_hfp_4647g`'s `4716–4725`, so
  the `0 px` edge frame itself is held without injecting an earlier frame's mask into a boundary latent step). Only if the entire
  shot has an empty mask on every frame does it fall back to real frames. Why: `second_hfp_4912e`, whose last frame (`4946`) had no
  mask and whose held copies filled the encoder's last clip with her untouched hair, and `lead_fp_2050c`, where dropping the hold
  back to real frames when an edge mask was small exposed unmasked context frames to the reference stream.
- The audio the model hears is silent under held frames; the audio muxed into the piece is the source's.

## 5. What is thrown away

After the decode, every padding frame is dropped (`audio_freeze_song.py::MiniMaxH3AudioFreezeSong.execute`), so the
piece, its region and its matte are the load's own frames; where a load inside continuous footage runs masked to the full legal window length (`pad_after == 0`), the extra denoised frames past the shot's piece are dropped at the lay by `laid.jsonl`'s `frames` field (`bench/delivery_table.py`).

## 6. Zoom Rules: Node Render-Region Zoom vs. `<Video 1>` Reference Crop Zoom

A masked V2V load has **two separate zoom systems** that operate on different streams for different reasons. Mixing them up or letting an outlier frame collapse either zoom degrades facial likeness, gaze, or plate sharpness.

### 6.1 Node Render-Region Zoom (`video_mask.py::zoom_box`, `zoomed_source`, `lay_back`)

Before the DiT sees the source video and masks, `video_mask.py::zoom_box` (`video_mask.py:463-619`) computes **one static canvas-aspect (`4:3`) bounding box** across all active masked frames of the load, crops the `1024x768` source and masks to that box (`video_mask.py::zoomed_source`), runs the DiT at `1024x768`, and composites the regenerated pixels back into the full frame through the feathered matte (`video_mask.py::lay_back`).

- **Constants and the 4 `set_by` limiters (`video_mask.py:419-549`)**:
  - `ZOOM_LARGEST = 2.0`: default `zoom_up_to` when a job does not ask for more (`set_by = "the largest zoom"`).
  - `ZOOM_MOST_ASKED = 4.0`: hard ceiling on `zoom_up_to` (`set_by = "the zoom any load may take"`).
  - `ZOOM_HEAD_TARGET = 300`: target head height in zoomed canvas pixels; caps zoom at `300 / median_head_height_px` (`set_by = "the head's target height"`) so a close-up head (`>= 240 px`) is not over-zoomed.
  - `ZOOM_ROOM = 0.25`: `25%` context padding kept around the union mask + dilated margin (`set_by = "the box"` when travel or mask width limits the zoom).
  - `ZOOM_SMALLEST = 1.25`: below `1.25x`, `zoom_box` returns `None` (`1.0x` whole frame) to avoid a pointless bilinear resample.
- **Settled per-subject recipes in `data/fun/plan.json` (enforced in code by `cbd39fbe` via `bench/shot_to_load.py` and `bench/flags_before_render.py::recipe_check_of`; updated 2026-10-30)**:
  - `lead`: `margin = 16`, `zoom_up_to = 4.0` (raised from `2.0` on 2026-10-30 after `lead_fp_4227c` at `1.44x` and `lead_fp_5085b` at `2.00x` left small background faces blurry; `ZOOM_HEAD_TARGET = 300` keeps close-up/medium heads `>= 150 px` at `<= 2.0x` while zooming small heads up to `4.0x`).
  - `second`: `margin = 64`, `zoom_up_to = 4.0` (shot `1093`: `margin = 128` for blonde hair room).
  - `third`: `margin = 16`, `zoom_up_to = 4.0` (raised from `2.0` on 2026-10-30 so small background faces zoom toward `ZOOM_HEAD_TARGET = 300`).
- **Three quality trade-offs every session must watch**:
  1. **"Zoom buys DiT tokens, not source pixel detail" (`video_mask.py:432-447`)**: The video loader fits the raw film (`1424x1068` on `fun`) down to the `1024x768` canvas *before* `zoom_box` crops and bilinearly upsamples it (`F.interpolate(..., mode="bilinear", align_corners=False)`). Thus a `4.0x` canvas zoom crops a `256x192` patch of the `1024x768` canvas (`~356x267` raw source pixels, a `2.88x` enlargement of raw film) up to `1024x768`. That gives a `40 px` distant face up to `~160 px` (`5` DiT tokens tall instead of `1.25`), which is decisive for eyes and teeth—**but** past `4.0x` the unmasked background plate in the zoom box becomes visibly bilinear-soft, and the DiT matches that softness inside the hole.
  2. **`margin` (`grow_pixels`) and `feather_pixels` are in zoomed pixels (`video_mask.py:604-608`) — why `third` must stay at `margin = 16`**: `dilate_mask(mask, grow_pixels)` runs *after* cropping to the zoom box (`margin = 64` at `2.46x` zoom = `26 px` on the whole `1024x768` canvas). For `second` (whose blonde hair needs room outside the dark source hair), `margin = 64` at `zoom_up_to = 4.0` is essential. For `third` (a face-only swap), raising `margin` to `64` or `96` (`third_fp_0000b/c`, `third_fp_0699b/c`) made the regenerated hole cover such a large fraction of the zoomed canvas that H3 copied `<Video 1>`'s white face-drawing outlines directly onto his skin; keeping `margin = 16, zoom_up_to = 2.0` (`third_fp_0000d`, `0699d`) eliminated the white lines completely.
  3. **Wide lateral travel suppresses static `zoom_box` (`set_by = "the box"`)**: Because `zoom_box` uses one static union rectangle across all active frames of the load, a subject who walks `> 512 px` horizontally across the frame forces the union box to span most of the `1024 px` width, dropping zoom to `1.0x` (e.g. `second` on `516–599`, where `516 px` of lateral travel left her face `35 px` = `1.1` DiT tokens tall at `1.0x` zoom). Raising `zoom_up_to` to `4.0` (and, only where each piece is `>= 39` frames or ends at a cut without leaving unmasked in-shot context frames, splitting at natural movement phases) restored `2.51x–3.88x` zoom (`108–122 px` = `3.4–3.8` DiT tokens).

### 6.2 `<Video 1>` Reference Crop Zoom (`bench/crop_reference_to_head.py::cranium_box`, `one_box`)

Independently of the node's render-region zoom, `bench/crop_reference_to_head.py` crops the `1024x768` 3D grey head + 2D facial-landmark video (`<Video 1>`) to a single static `4:3` box (`one_box`) and resamples it to `1024x768` before feeding Qwen3-VL (`16 px` patches merged to `32 px`) and `motion_vae` (`32 px` DiT tokens).

- **Crop modes (`bench/head_reference.py::CROP_FORMS`)**:
  - `head`: union of SAM `head_track` + Sapiens face with `HEAD_ROOM = 0.25` (includes big hair volume, drawing the face smaller).
  - `face`: union of 2D face keypoints with `FACE_BROW_LIFT = 0.60` (tight on the face, can clip the 3D cranium).
  - `cranium` (**settled default for all subjects in `data/fun/plan.json`**): frames SAM 3D Body's bald grey head (`bench/crop_reference_to_head.py::grey_head` above mid-face so neck/shoulders/hands below the chin do not bloat the box) + Sapiens `upper_face` (glabella to nose tip), padded by `CRANIUM_ROOM = 0.15` above/beside and `CRANIUM_NECK = 0.35` below the chin, capped at **`CRANIUM_MOST = 5.75`** (`bench/crop_reference_to_head.py:40-44`) so resampling a `1024x768` render does not blur the 1–2 px landmark lines.
- **Why `<Video 1>` zoom governs gaze**: At `1.654x` (`head` crop on `second_0278_0293_g`), a drawn iris is `15–21 px` wide and, when clipped by downcast lids, only `6–9 px` tall—under a single `16 px` Qwen3-VL patch (`claude-face-crop` measurement, 2026-10-22). `cranium` maximizes the iris size across both Qwen3-VL and `motion_vae`.
- **Automatic 4-corner `head_track` containment & outlier guard (`dc07ccee`, `b7875bf1`, `7ae92c9b`)**:
  - On `lead_fp_0294c` (frames `317–319`), a 3-frame foreground occlusion produced a bogus `389 px` 2D face box (`score 0.04`) touching an inverted 3D body mesh (`385x321 px`), which bloated the whole-shot `one_box` union and collapsed `<Video 1>` zoom from **`2.592x` to `1.517x`** across all 121 frames.
  - In `bench/crop_reference_to_head.py::cranium_box` (`bench/crop_reference_to_head.py:221-303`), every frame's 2D `face_rect` (`x0, y0, x1, y1`, **all 4 corners**) must now lie inside the `50%`-grown SAM 3.1 `head_track` window (`CRANIUM_HEAD_TRACK_GROW = 0.50`, `bench/crop_reference_to_head.py:46-50`) and its grey-head slice is clipped to that window (`grey_head(..., window=win[k])`); when no `head_track` is present, frames exceeding `CRANIUM_FACE_OUTLIER = 2.00` times the active median face height are rejected (`form="track"` fallback).
  - **What to pass on occluded or mesh-corrupted spans**: Whenever a 3D mesh flips, crouches (`lead_fp_3595c` frames `3613–3625, 3636–3649`), or loses track during an occlusion, pass `--no-head-on FIRST-LAST=WHY` (default `form="hold"`, which excludes those frames from `one_box` union sizing and holds the last clean reference frame) and `--drop` for single-frame pose spikes (`bench/audit_reference_clip.py`, [`upstream_anomaly_and_alignment_checks.md`](upstream_anomaly_and_alignment_checks.md)).

## 7. Best Practices for Drawing Faces, Mouths, and Eyes/Gaze on `<Video 1>`

`<Video 1>` (`bench/draw_face_points.py` + `bench/head_reference.py`) and `<Subject 2>`'s prompt text (`bench/load_text.py`) are tightly coupled: **never change a mouth or eye drawing mode without checking the prompt clause `bench/load_text.py` emits for it** ([`2026-10-29_findings_0278n_text_and_frame_table.md`](2026-10-29_findings_0278n_text_and_frame_table.md), [`reference_and_prompt_rules.md`](reference_and_prompt_rules.md)).

### 7.1 Closed Mouths: What a Closed Mouth Looks Like (`sculpted` vs. `outlined`)

Sapiens2 (`sapiens2-seg-1b` + `sapiens2-pose-1b`) calls a frame's mouth **closed** whenever the 29-class map has no Teeth (`26`) or Tongue (`27`) hole OR the vertical inner-lip gap (`93..95` to `99..101`) is `<= bench/draw_face_points.py::INNER_GAP_CLOSED_PX` (`3.0 px`). There are **two legal closed-mouth forms** in `bench/draw_face_points.py::CLOSED_MOUTH_FORMS`:

| Closed-Mouth Form | What Is Drawn on `<Video 1>` | When to Use It | Why / What Breaks If Misused |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`sculpted`** *(Default; mandatory on 100% silent shots where `open_frames == []`)* | **No line and no shape drawn at all (`mouths[f] = None`).** The 3D body model's natural sculpted grey mesh lips are left completely untouched. | Default for `lead` and `third` on all shots, and **mandatory for `second` on any silent shot (`open_frames == []`, e.g. `0278–0293`)** where her mouth stays closed and neutral. | 1. **Why not a pose-point parting line?** Pose keypoints (`91..110`) sit `8–12 px` above the 3D mesh's sculpted grey lips (`second_hfp_0278d/e/h`), creating a double mouth that H3 renders as parted/open lips.<br>2. **Why not a flat grey oval (`smooth_mouth_area`, retired)?** Painting a flat grey patch over the 3D shaded lips creates a darker oval splotch (`second_hfp_0415e`, `0484d`, `1039b`) that H3 reads as an open mouth.<br>3. **Why mandatory on silent shots (`open_frames == []`)**: In `bench/load_text.py::mouth_drawn` (`bench/load_text.py:214-218`), `mouth_drawn = mouth_drawn_open or mouth_drawn_closed`. Setting `closed_mouth: "outlined"` on a silent shot (`second_hfp_0278n`) flips `mouth_drawn` to `True` and emits `"how far the mouth opens are transferred"` + `"her mouth opening and closing where its mouth does"`, causing H3 to open her mouth wide across a silent close-up! |
| **`outlined`** *(`second`'s recipe on shots with singing or closed-lip smile/frown expression)* | Draws the **outer lip ring + a flat skin-grey interior (`lips_paint_out`) + a single center meeting line with NO dark interior** (`bench/draw_face_points.py::anchored_closed_mouth`, `CLOSED_MOUTHS`), placed on Sapiens2 class-map lips via `onto_the_lips` (never `fit_mouth_shapes_to_head`), capped to `MOUTH_WIDTH_MOST = 0.95` and `MOUTH_CLOSED_HEIGHT_MOST = 0.35` of `upper_face`, with `MOUTH_BEND` corner lift (`smile` / `frown`) when width `>= 20 px` and corner score `>= 0.5`. | `second` on shots where she sings (`open_frames > 0`) or where her closed-lip expression must override `<Picture 1>`'s smile (e.g. `5114k`). | Without `outlined` on `second_hfp_5114i` (`open_frames == []`), drawing nothing at the mouth let `<Picture 1>`'s broad smile bleed through (`decisions.md`, 2026-10-23). Note: `outlined` is **never** passed through `fit_mouth_shapes_to_head` (which pushed closed mouths under the nose or onto the cheek on profile shots `4479` and `5114`); it stays on the Sapiens2 class-map lips and is clipped to `find_mesh_chin_y` inside `HEAD_SILHOUETTE_ERODE_PX = 2`. |

### 7.2 Open Mouths (`inner outline` vs. `outlined`)

When Sapiens2 detects a Teeth/Tongue hole AND an inner-lip gap `> INNER_GAP_CLOSED_PX` (`3.0 px`), `bench/draw_face_points.py` draws an open mouth (`bench/draw_face_points.py::OPEN_MOUTH_FORMS`):

- **`inner outline` (Default for `second` and `third`)**: Fills the inner-lip polygon (`91..110`) solid black (`0, 0, 0`) with a white outline (`inner_lip_ring`), fitted into the 3D grey head's mouth-to-chin space via `bench/draw_face_points.py::fit_mouth_to_head` (`min_chin_margin = max(8.0 px, 0.14 * (chin_y - eye_y))` inside `HEAD_SILHOUETTE_ERODE_PX = 2`).
- **`outlined` (`lead`'s settled recipe in `data/fun/plan.json`, 2026-10-22)**: Places both the outer lip outline and inner lip outline onto the Sapiens2 class-map lips (`bench/draw_face_points.py::anchored_mouth`, `onto_the_lips`), paints the 3D mesh lips flat skin-grey inside the outer ring (`lips_paint_out`), caps the dark interior to at most **`MOUTH_OPEN_SHARE_MOST = 0.50`** of the outer mouth height, caps total width to **`MOUTH_WIDTH_MOST = 0.95`** and opening height to **`MOUTH_OPENING_MOST = 0.50`** of `upper_face` (glabella to nose bottom), applies `MOUTH_BEND` corner lift, and fits the shapes to the 3D grey head via `bench/draw_face_points.py::fit_mouth_shapes_to_head` so an open singing mouth never exaggerates into a giant black cavern (`lead_fp_1156d`) or hangs off the jaw as a dark goatee (`lead_fp_0099j`).

### 7.3 Eye Gaze, Iris, Downcast Eyes, and Shut Eyes

Every eye drawing decision is governed by how H3's VAE (`32 px` tokens) and Qwen3-VL (`16 px` patches) read contrast inside the eyelid slit (`bench/draw_face_points.py:113-220`):

| Eye State | Code Rule & Constants in `bench/draw_face_points.py` | What Is Drawn on `<Video 1>` | Why / Failure Mode Prevented |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Open Eyes (`--iris contrast`, default)** | `IRIS_FORMS = ("arc", "contrast")` (`REFERENCE_DEFAULT` uses `"contrast"`). | **2. Downcast / Lowered Gaze (`>= 65%` of iris outside lids)** | `DOWNCAST_RING_OUTSIDE_MIN = 0.65` (`bench/draw_face_points.py:113-117`) + `EYE_OPEN = {"least": 0.12, "way": "down"}` (`bench/draw_face_points.py:119-135`, `dark_irises`, `open_lids`). Fires automatically with no CLI flag. | **No white sclera fill** (`dark_irises` drops the white sclera and draws a solid black iris disc clipped to the lids), and if the lid slit area over width squared is `< 0.12`, **`open_lids` pulls the lower lid downward** so at least `12%` of width is open and a piece of the black iris disc is visible. | Prevents two opposite bugs:<br>1. **White-slit camera stare**: Flooding a narrow downcast slit with `255, 255, 255` white sclera overpowers the tiny iris so H3 renders wide-open eyes staring at the camera.<br>2. **Missing black iris -> `<Picture 1>` fallback**: If the lids are so narrow that the white lid stroke covers the black iris (`0278j/k`), H3 sees no black iris on frame 1 and defaults to `<Picture 1>`'s straight-ahead open eyes (*"if frame 1 can't see a black iris, it defaults to the ref image eyes"*, 2026-10-22). |
| **3. Shut Eyes (`--eyes-shut-on` + `--eyes-shut-form closed` + shut-eye prompt text)** | `EYES_SHUT_FORMS = ("none", "lids", "lowered", "closed")` (`bench/draw_face_points.py:136-162`, `sculpted_eye`, `SCULPTED_EYE`). | **`closed`**: `sculpted_eye` finds SAM 3D Body's 3D sculpted eye socket around the lash ring, **paints the entire 3D eye almond flat in surrounding `skin_grey`** (hard-edged, inside the head silhouette), and draws **one single closed white curve along the upper lash line, with NO iris** (brows stay). **Must be paired with a shut-eye sentence in `<Subject 2>` / `[Shot 1]` (`text --by-hand`, modeled on `bench/load_text.py::subject_2`'s `lids` `shut_tail` at `bench/load_text.py:314-331`).** | 1. **Why `--eyes-shut-form lowered` fails when `share_outside >= 0.65` (`second_hfp_0278m`)**: `lowered` only forces `dark_irises`, which `DOWNCAST_RING_OUTSIDE_MIN = 0.65` *already* triggered—and `open_lids` still props the lower lid down to `0.12`, producing a pixel-identical `<Video 1>` drawing with visible dark irises!<br>2. **Why `closed` requires matching prompt text (`second_hfp_0278n` vs. `0278o`)**: In `0278n`, `closed` painted the eye out on `<Video 1>`, but `bench/load_text.py::FACE_EYES` still told H3 *"the eyes, each with a dark iris, open, close and look..."*, so H3 saw no iris and fell back to `<Picture 1>`'s open eyes.
