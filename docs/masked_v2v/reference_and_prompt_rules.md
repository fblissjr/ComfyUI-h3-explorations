## 1. Mouth Drawing Rules (Closed = 3D Gray Mesh Lips by Default; Open = Black Interior + White Outline)

- **No mouth smoothing (`smooth_mouth_area` retired, 2026-10-20)**: `bench/draw_face_points.py::smooth_mouth_area` is no longer called on the drawing path (still defined, and exercised only by `bench/check_draw_face_points.py`). Painting a flat gray oval over the mouth region created a bright gray splotch across the lips, chin, and hands (`second_hfp_0415e`, `second_hfp_0484d`, `second_hfp_1039b`) that H3 read as an open mouth.
- **Closed-mouth frames stay as natural 3D gray mesh lips (`mouths[f] = None`)** by default: Whenever Sapiens2 (`sapiens2-seg-1b` + `sapiens2-pose-1b`) detects a closed mouth (`(not has_hole) or (gap <= INNER_GAP_CLOSED_PX)`, `bench/draw_face_points.py::INNER_GAP_CLOSED_PX`), `bench/draw_face_points.py::run` sets `mouths[f] = None` and draws no mouth there, leaving the 3D gray mesh's sculpted closed lips untouched, unless the subject's plan says `reference.closed_mouth: "outlined"` (below). **On a 100% silent shot where `open_frames == []` and her mouth is neutral (e.g. `0278–0293`), `closed_mouth` must stay `"sculpted"` (never `"outlined"`)**: in `bench/load_text.py::mouth_drawn` (`bench/load_text.py:214-218`), `mouth_drawn = mouth_drawn_open or mouth_drawn_closed`, so setting `closed_mouth: "outlined"` on a silent shot (`second_hfp_0278n`) flips `mouth_drawn` to `True` and emits `"how far the mouth opens are transferred"` + `"her mouth opening and closing where its mouth does"`, causing H3 to open her mouth wide across a silent close-up ([`2026-10-29_findings_0278n_text_and_frame_table.md`](2026-10-29_findings_0278n_text_and_frame_table.md)).
- **Open-mouth frames draw black interior + white outline (`inner_lip_ring` / `mouth_inner`)**: Whenever Sapiens2 detects an open mouth (a teeth/tongue hole in the 29-class map and an inner-lip gap over `bench/draw_face_points.py::INNER_GAP_CLOSED_PX`), the black interior + white outline is drawn at the mouth, and `bench/draw_face_points.py::fit_mouth_to_head` keeps the drawn mouth inside the gray head's mouth-to-chin space (its docstring says how).
- **An open mouth's form is the plan's, a subject at a time** (`reference.open_mouth`, `bench/draw_face_points.py::OPEN_MOUTH_FORMS`; the owner, 2026-10-22, on `lead_fp_1156d`). Without it, `inner outline`: the whole inner-lip ring filled black. The lead's is `outlined`: the lips' outer outline and the inner one round a dark inside, placed on the Sapiens2 class map's lips (not the pose points, and not above them), the whole mouth no wider than `bench/draw_face_points.py::MOUTH_WIDTH_MOST` and no more open than `MOUTH_OPENING_MOST` of the upper face (glabella to the bottom of the nose, so a close-up and a far shot keep one proportion), the dark inside at most `MOUTH_OPEN_SHARE_MOST` of the outer lips, the body model's lips painted flat exactly inside the outer outline (`anchored_mouth`, `lips_paint_out`). A frame whose class map holds no lips takes its nearest neighbour's. `capped` and `outlined over the lips` are built, no subject's form. Closed frames are the same in every open form. The measures behind each limit are in the constants' comments.
- **A closed mouth's form is the plan's too, a subject at a time** (`reference.closed_mouth` with the reference's `why`, `bench/draw_face_points.py::CLOSED_MOUTH_FORMS`; the owner, 2026-10-23, on `second_hfp_5114i`: where the closed-lip line called every frame closed nothing was drawn at the mouth and the text named none, and the second subject took the still's smile, "If we dont have a drawing for mouth expressions or facial expressions like that we probably do need one"). Without it, `sculpted`: nothing drawn, as above. `outlined`: on each frame the line calls closed, the lips' outer outline and the line where they meet (`anchored_closed_mouth`), placed on the class map's lips by the outlined open mouth's own move (`onto_the_lips`, so not on the pose points, the 0278d/e/h fault), no wider than `MOUTH_WIDTH_MOST` nor taller than `MOUTH_CLOSED_HEIGHT_MOST` of the upper face, the body model's lips painted flat inside the outline (`lips_paint_out`), the meeting line with no dark edge (`CLOSED_MOUTHS`). It stays on the class map's lips: it is not fitted to the grey head (the build's blind read of 2026-10-23: the open form's `fit_mouth_shapes_to_head` moved it to a line under the nose or onto the cheek on the profile shots 4479-4509 and 5114-5129, the 0278 fault), only cut, as every mark is, to the head's silhouette above the chin `bench/draw_face_points.py::find_mesh_chin_y` finds; a frame where none of it is left is not drawn and the record lists it (`frames_not_drawn_nothing_on_the_head`). Under the same word every drawn mouth, open or closed, takes the source's expression as a bend (`bench/draw_face_points.py::MOUTH_BEND`, `bend_mouth`; the owner, 2026-10-23): its corners drawn above its middle (a smile) or below (a frown) by the corners' lift on that frame (`corner_lift`, on the pose model's points read at the canvas), flat inside a dead zone, and no bend at all where the corners are not read (a mouth narrower than `least_width_px` at the canvas, or a corner under `least_corner_score`: on 4479-4509 and 5114-5129 the figure was noise, on 0014-0167 it followed raised corners; the constant's comment has the measures). The pose points set only the bend: every point keeps its place across, the middle and the thickness stay where the class map's lips put them, an open mouth is bent after its fit to the grey head and its dark inside keeps its height; open frames are otherwise drawn in their own form. It reaches the drawing as `head_reference --closed-mouth` (`bench/shot_to_load.py::drawn_form`, under the closed-lip line only), and the record's `drawn.mouth.closed_lip_line.closed_mouth` lists `frames_drawn_closed` (only frames where some of it is left) with each frame's drawn size over the upper face, its box against the class map lips' box and how far the fit would have moved it, and under `bend` each frame's reading, the frames bent and how far the bend moved each. On a silent window (mouth `left out`) nothing is drawn at the mouth and the record says so. Whether it holds her mouth as the source's is a render's to decide. On a turned head `find_mesh_chin_y` can find the grey head's edge just under the nose, and there the cut leaves nothing (5115-5117 and 5120-5124 of 5114 in the build's proof).

## 2. Eye Drawing Rules (Contrast Iris, Downcast Dark Iris, and Shut Eyes)

- **A lowered eye is drawn with a dark iris, and a closed eye is painted over** (2026-10-22; the iris arc between the lids from 2026-10-20). Under `--iris contrast` with the closed lip line, the chain's default form (`bench/shot_to_load.py::REFERENCE_DEFAULT`), an eye whose iris ring lies `bench/draw_face_points.py::DOWNCAST_RING_OUTSIDE_MIN` or more outside its lids is drawn dark with no person's word: its lash ring, eyelid and crease lines, no white sclera, and the iris a solid black disc clipped to the lids, so a narrow eye shows a dark iris where the pose model puts it (`dark_irises` in [`bench/draw_face_points.py`](../../bench/draw_face_points.py)); an eye under the share keeps the white and black contrast form.
- A dark iris is drawn in lids no thinner than `bench/draw_face_points.py::EYE_OPEN` (its `least`, the lash ring's area over the eye's width squared, measured on frames whose gaze rendered right): where the pose model's lids are thinner, the drawn lower lid is opened down to it, the corners and the upper lid kept (`open_lids`), so a piece of the black iris always shows (after 0278k, a second seed, looked up as 0278j did); the record lists the frames (`drawn.eye_rings.frames_the_lids_are_opened_on`). The lid opens downward (`EYE_OPEN`'s `way`, the owner's choice of 2026-10-22). The cost: an eye shut in the source is drawn open too; name a shut stretch with `--eyes-shut-on ... --eyes-shut-form closed` and a text sentence that says the eyes are shut (`text --by-hand` until `bench/load_text.py` has one for the form; the `lids` clause is the model): `closed` paints the sculpted eye out and the template's sentence still says a dark iris that opens and closes, and on `second_hfp_0278n` (2026-10-29) H3 took the still's straight-ahead eyes, whereas on `second_hfp_0278o` (`closed` + `closed_mouth: "sculpted"` + shut-eye prompt text) her mouth stayed shut `16/16` frames and her eyes stayed shut across `278–286` (`9/16` frames; `<Picture 1>` + the upward 3D head tilt pulled them open on `287–293`). `lowered` is not the answer where the downcast rule already fired: on `278–293` it draws the same propped-open lids and dark discs `0278m` rendered open eyes from; see [`2026-10-29_findings_0278n_text_and_frame_table.md`](2026-10-29_findings_0278n_text_and_frame_table.md). On 0278m the gaze held down, then opened as her head lifted; that is parked, not solved ([`docs/wiki/decisions.md`](../wiki/decisions.md), 2026-10-22). The reference record says, eye by eye, which frames were drawn dark (`drawn.eye_rings.frames_an_iris_is_drawn_dark_on`).
- A person's word (`job --eyes-shut-on`, with `--eyes-shut-form` from `bench/draw_face_points.py::EYES_SHUT_FORMS`) draws its frames `none`, `lids`, `lowered` (every eye dark, whatever the share) or `closed` (the body model's whole sculpted eye, found on its frame round the lash ring by `bench/draw_face_points.py::sculpted_eye` and `SCULPTED_EYE`, painted flat in the skin grey round it, hard-edged and never past the head's edge, then one white line along the upper lash line, no iris; the brows stay). The text has no words of its own for `lowered` or `closed` (`lids` and `none` each add a clause): `<Subject 2>` says "the eyes, each with a dark iris, open, close and look where the person's eyes do" (`bench/load_text.py::FACE_EYES`), which the dark iris on downcast and `lowered` frames makes true there too, where the iris arc it replaced had no black. Drawing lowered eyes shut by rule was tried on 2026-10-21 and taken back out the same day: H3 drew her eyes open and toward the camera on `second_hfp_0484g` and `second_hfp_1039e` ([`docs/wiki/decisions.md`](../wiki/decisions.md)).

## 3. Reference Crop Modes (`head`, `face`, `cranium`)

- **The reference is cut to the head's box, or to the face's or the grey head's on the plan's word** (`reference.crop`, `bench/head_reference.py::CROP_FORMS`; 2026-10-22, after 0278j/k looked the wrong way on the head box where 0278f, on the face's, did not; see [`frames_rules.md`](frames_rules.md) §6.2 for how reference crop zoom compares to node render zoom).
- `head`, the default since `ba061ef3`, holds the head track with room above the crown and the faces, so her whole head and hair are in `<Video 1>` and the face is drawn smaller.
- `face` sizes the one box on the faces alone with `bench/crop_reference_to_head.py::HEAD_ROOM` and reads no head track: the face larger, the crown possibly cut. Larger is not enough on its own: on 278-293 the drawn iris stays under one Qwen3-VL 16 px patch tall on any of the three boxes (the cranium crop's iris is larger and still under one patch; [`docs/wiki/decisions.md`](../wiki/decisions.md), 2026-10-22).
- `cranium` (the Gemini reviewer's design, 2026-10-22) frames the body model's own bald grey head, since that is all `<Video 1>` holds, not the real hair the head track holds: each frame's face with the grey joined to it above mid-face (`bench/crop_reference_to_head.py::grey_head`; above the chin the neck and shoulders join raised arms and risen shoulders, seen on lead 1156 and second 0278), `CRANIUM_ROOM` above and beside, `CRANIUM_NECK` below the chin, one box over the load in the canvas's shape, enlarged at most `CRANIUM_MOST`; the record's `crop.cranium` says on which frames the grey head reaches the box's edge and what the subject's travel costs the enlargement, as figures. The record's `crop` says the form, the why and the enlargement; the text says "a closer view of the head and shoulders" in every form (`bench/load_text.py::subject_2`). Every subject of `fun` takes `cranium` (the plan; the owner, 2026-10-22). By `dc07ccee` (`2026-10-28`, after a 3-frame occlusion on `lead_fp_0294c` frames `317–319` blew up the 2D face box to `389 px` and inverted the 3D mesh, dragging whole-shot zoom down from `2.592x` to `1.517x`), `bench/crop_reference_to_head.py::cranium_box` enforces 4-corner SAM 3.1 `head_track` window containment (`CRANIUM_HEAD_TRACK_GROW = 0.50`) and clips `grey_head` to that grown window (or filters frames exceeding `CRANIUM_FACE_OUTLIER = 2.00` times the active median face height when no `head_track` is present), falling back to `head_track` (`form="track"`) on rejected frames; on occluded or mesh-corrupted spans, pass `--no-head-on FIRST-LAST=WHY` (default `form="hold"`, which excludes those frames from `one_box` union sizing and freezes the last clean frame). A frame whose face has no grey head joined to it does not size the cranium box; a span with no grey head at all keeps those frames (`crop.cranium.those_frames_size_the_box`).

## 4. Unified `<Subject 2>` and `<Subject 1>` Prompt Text (`bench/load_text.py`)

- **Single unified `<Subject 2>` prompt sentence (`bench/load_text.py::subject_2`)**: under the closed-lip line every face-drawn load's `<Subject 2>` sentence ends "with its face drawn as thin white outlines: the eyes, each with a dark iris, open, close and look where the person's eyes do" (`bench/load_text.py::FACE_EYES`; the owner's approval of 2026-10-22; until then "with facial features drawn on its face as thin white outlines, where the eyes open, close and look where the person's eyes do", which a load that draws no eye still says, `FACE_NO_EYES`). It names the mouth only for the outlined form (`bench/load_text.py::MOUTH_CLAUSE`) where the drawing opens a mouth on some frame (`bench/load_text.py::mouth_drawn_open`: the closed-lip line's `open_frames`, the owner's go of 2026-10-22): "; where the mouth is open, the lips are outlined and it is dark inside, and the mouth opens and closes where the person's mouth does", tied to the open mouth because a closed frame draws nothing. Until 2026-10-22 no load's sentence named the mouth.
- **The rest of the text is the owner's form of 2026-10-22** (`second_hfp_0415l`, written by hand and kept): `<Subject 1>`'s definition names the hair `<Picture 1>` shows (the plan's `text.picture_hair`) and `[Shot 1]` the hair the shot ends up with (`text.hair_words`), never eye, eyebrow or skin words (the plan's `looks` is read by no line); a face-only subject's retention line is "the girl's face from <Picture 1> is retained."; a face-and-hair subject reads "the face and the <picture_hair> of <Picture 1> are retained.", its summary "with the face and the <picture_hair> of <Picture 1>" and `[Shot 1]` "has the face and the hair of <Picture 1>" (the plan's `text.picture_hair`; the owner's word of 2026-10-23, which also retired the plan's `text.hair_change`, refused if a plan still carries it); where a face is drawn, `<Subject 2>`'s retention line transfers "the movements of the head, the facial expressions, where the eyes look and how far the mouth opens" and `[Shot 1]` ends "..., her eyes looking where its eyes look and her mouth opening and closing where its mouth does." (`bench/load_text.py::TRANSFERRED_DRAWN`, `FOLLOWS_DRAWN`, the owner's approval of 2026-10-22), and where the drawing draws no mouth on any frame (`bench/load_text.py::mouth_drawn`: none opened, `mouth_drawn_open`, and none drawn closed under the plan's `closed_mouth`, `mouth_drawn_closed`, 2026-10-23) they name no mouth: "the movements of the head, the facial expressions and where the eyes look" and "..., her eyes looking where its eyes look." (`TRANSFERRED_DRAWN_NO_MOUTH`, `FOLLOWS_DRAWN_NO_MOUTH`, the owner's go of 2026-10-22 after `second_hfp_0278l`, whose drawing opened no mouth while its text said her mouth opens and closes, and she spoke; the singing line is the voice's and stays; "and eye movements" on a plain cut, the head's movements alone on a hair load); the music is `N/A`. The one home of the form is [`bench/load_text.py`](../../bench/load_text.py) (its docstring), held byte for byte to the owner's text by `bench/check_load_text.py`.
- **What each form did on a render** is the owner's to judge on playback: their verdicts are in `data/<video>/owner_answers.jsonl` and `data/<video>/verdicts/verdicts.jsonl`, and what was decided and corrected is in [`docs/wiki/decisions.md`](../wiki/decisions.md) (2026-10-20, item 6, corrected on 2026-10-21).
