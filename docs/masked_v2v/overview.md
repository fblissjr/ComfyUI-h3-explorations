# Masked video to video: the lane in one page

**The lane replaces one person in an existing video, from a reference still, and keeps everything else**: the rest of
the picture and the video's own audio. A masked region is regenerated by H3; everything outside it is frozen
context. This is one use of H3, not the pack (root [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md), "The repo is broader than the
current project").

- **Rules first**: [`data/AGENTS.md`](../../data/AGENTS.md) holds the lane's settled rules, the one ledger and where each
  video's state lives. Each video's state is its own `data/<video>/AGENTS.md`. These pages hold no video's state.
- **Where a page and the code disagree, the code is right.** A value is named by the constant that holds it, a result
  by the dated record that holds it.

## What it is

**It is an inference-time method, not a trained task.** The release trains t2va, fl2va and ref2va. The mechanism is
core's: a latent noise mask on H3 is a per-token timestep (`comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py::mask_row_values`), and the
clean latent is put back into the kept tokens every step. "Clean" here means the clean latent mixed with noise at the
level core uses for a reference (`comfy/model_base.py::MiniMaxH3.scale_latent_inpaint`). So a text tells the model who
is generated in each hole and from which reference, never the frozen picture ([`text.md`](text.md)).

**How a job is built: by the chain, not from a shipped graph.** `bench/shot_to_load.py` and `bench/load_chain.py`
build every load's graphs through `workflows/masked_job_graph.py` ([`running_a_job.md`](running_a_job.md)). The
shipped graphs are the same lane as single graphs to open by hand:

| graph (`workflows/`) | daily copy (`h3_config.DAILY_GRAPHS`) | for |
|---|---|---|
| `h3_video_to_video_masked_song_pdd8_api.json` | `daily/h3_mask_pdd8_api.json` | the default: a video loader, the SAM 3 checkpoint, the three pack nodes (who, what happens to them, the prompt) and the PDD8 song chain with Sol-Attn; for a shot that needs no movement from the source |

| `h3_video_to_video_masked_review_api.json` | `daily/h3_mask_review_api.json` | the look before a render: the song node on `preview`, nothing sampled; the tracker's numbered tiles and shot table saved, the region and the prompt shown. | `h3_video_to_video_masked_parts_song_pdd8_api.json` | `daily/h3_mask_parts_pdd8_api.json` | a part of the subject Sapiens2 finds (`MiniMaxH3Sapiens2Loader`, `MiniMaxH3SubjectParts` into the Masked Source's `parts`; `replace` is `the wired parts`); at the part node's own ticks, hair, face and neck on the original's body. ## How a job runs

One load is one subject on one shot. The steps, in order; the data each reads and writes is
[`pipeline.md`](pipeline.md), and the commands for a day are [`docs/working/runbook.md`](../working/runbook.md).

| step | what happens | tool | detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | the video gets an analysis folder and contact sheets | `bench/clip_analysis.py init`, `bench/contact_sheets.py` | [`pipeline.md`](pipeline.md) |
| 2 | a no-sampling preview of each shot: a SAM 3.1 track a person, parts, class maps, pose, body mesh | `bench/shot_to_load.py preview` | [`sam31.md`](sam31.md), [`pieces.md`](pieces.md) |
| 3 | a session reads the tiles, the owner answers who is who, the cast file keeps it | `bench/who_page.py`, `bench/page_answers_to_cast.py` | [`who_is_who.md`](who_is_who.md) |
| 4 | the plan says each person's region, parts, give-backs and voice | `data/<video>/plan.json` | [`data/AGENTS.md`](../../data/AGENTS.md) |
| 5 | the load's job file, with every frame of the shot read first | `bench/shot_to_load.py job` | [`running_a_job.md`](running_a_job.md), "A shot, made whole" |
| 6 | the reference video: the body model cut to the head with the face points drawn | `bench/shot_to_load.py reference`, `bench/head_reference.py` | [`renders_by_form.md`](renders_by_form.md) |
| 7 | the text, from the reference's record and the plan | `bench/shot_to_load.py text`, `bench/load_text.py` | [`text.md`](text.md) |
| 8 | the capture and its gate, then the node's own plan, then a second reader's look | `bench/load_chain.py first`, `plan`; `bench/capture_masked_run.py looked` | [`working_rules.md`](working_rules.md), "The rule: data before a render, and the same data after" |
| 9 | the text read in full from the queued graph, then the render | `bench/what_h3_got.py`, `bench/load_chain.py render` | [`working_rules.md`](working_rules.md) |
| 10 | land, the owner's review, and the cut made new | `bench/load_chain.py land`, `bench/rebuild_delivery.py` | [`data/AGENTS.md`](../../data/AGENTS.md), "One ledger, made new on every landing" |

When a render is wrong, read its text, its frames, its mask and its audio side by side before anything else:
[`working_rules.md`](working_rules.md), "Reading one window whole".

## The pieces, in brief

| piece | node (file) | what it decides | detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `MiniMaxH3SubjectTrack` (`subject_track.py`) | which pixels are the person, shot by shot | [`pieces.md`](pieces.md); SAM 3.1 itself: [`sam31.md`](sam31.md) |
| 2 | `MiniMaxH3MaskedSource` (`video_mask.py`) | the region in whole tokens, what is kept, the motion video, the zoom, the composite | [`pieces.md`](pieces.md) |
| 3 | `MiniMaxH3MaskedPrompt` (`masked_prompt.py`) | the text for a shipped graph (a job's text is `bench/load_text.py`'s) | [`pieces.md`](pieces.md), [`text.md`](text.md) |
| 4 | the song node (`audio_freeze_song.py`) | each window regenerated with the track's audio frozen, its plan and its report | [`pieces.md`](pieces.md); windows: [`window_context.md`](window_context.md) |

Two facts every piece lives with: a latent step is several frames and its grid is fixed for a whole load
([`pieces.md`](pieces.md), "A latent step is several frames, and its grid is fixed for a whole load"); and nothing is
kept between runs by a key (the same page).
