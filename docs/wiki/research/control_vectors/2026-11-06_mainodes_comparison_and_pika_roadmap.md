## 1. Executive Summary & Architectural Comparison

Both `coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/` and our control vector stack (`control_vectors.py`, `control_vector_nodes.py`, `h3_spans.py`, `bench/control_vectors.py`) steer MiniMax H3 at the same primary bottleneck: the **5376-d post-refiner text conditioning tensor** (`context` of shape `[1, L, 5376]` after `condition_proj` and the 2-block bidirectional `token_refiner`) inside a `WrappersMP.DIFFUSION_MODEL` wrapper (`control_vector_nodes.py:1025-1114` vs. `coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/core.py:92-108`). Both accumulate stacked vectors in `float64` before casting to the context dtype and support Gram-Schmidt hold projection.

Beyond that shared seam, the two codebases make fundamentally different choices in **token-span pooling, inference row masking, checkpoint family & LoRA verification, extraction mechanics, and DiT velocity modulation**:

| Axis | `coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes` (`74cbd0e`, `v1.2.3`) | `ComfyUI-h3-explorations` (`control_vectors.py`, `control_vector_nodes.py`, `h3_spans.py`) |

## 2. What Methods We Do Better That Adopting `MAINodes`' Approach Would Break

Several negative results reported in `coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/LESSONS.md`—including *"zero_gravity ... did not show"* (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/LESSONS.md:14`), *"Today's post hook shifts every row of the text span, vision-pad rows included"* (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/LESSONS.md:68`), and *"On Pika, the prompt only matters in the first steps. Late envelopes at +2 and even +8 changed nothing"* (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/LESSONS.md:103-105`)—turned out in our Pika verification suite (`Arms 1..66` in [`bench/results/2026-11-05_control_vectors_pika_and_ref2va.md`](../../../../bench/results/2026-11-05_control_vectors_pika_and_ref2va.md)) to be artifacts of whole-prompt `.mean(0)` pooling, unmasked `all_rows` injection, and short 1-sentence prompts rather than limitations of H3's post-refiner seam.

Replacing any of the five core layers below with `MAINodes`' implementation would break verified invariants in `bench/check_control_vectors.py` (`C1–C7`) and degrade Pika `fl2va` and `ref2va` renders:

### 2.1 Token Pooling & Nuisance Removal (`diff_span` vs. Whole-Prompt `.mean(0)` + `NUISANCE_PAIRS` SVD)
- **What happens under whole-prompt `.mean(0)` (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/core.py:433`)**:
  - Stage 1 of H3's text conditioner (`MiniMaxQwen3VL`, `comfy/text_encoders/minimax.py:89-108`) is strictly causal (`is_causal = True`). Every token before `first_diff` is bitwise identical in Prompt A and Prompt B at the encoder (`||Delta e|| = 0.0000`) and near-zero after the 2-block `token_refiner` ([`docs/wiki/control_vectors.md`](../../control_vectors.md)).
  - On a full 120–180-token 3-section H3 prompt where an 8–12-word clause differs, averaging over all tokens dilutes `raw_norm` by **`2.1x–8.2x`** (`head_swell` drops from `85.01` under `diff_span` to `23.57` under `from_first_diff` and `10.35` under `scene_text`, [`bench/results/2026-11-05_control_vectors_pika_and_ref2va.md`](../../../../bench/results/2026-11-05_control_vectors_pika_and_ref2va.md)).
  - To compensate for short-caption elaboration drift under whole-prompt `.mean(0)`, `MAINodes` encodes 24 hardcoded 1-sentence pairs (`NUISANCE_PAIRS`, `coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/core.py:63-71`: `"A photo of A woman." ||| "A detailed, carefully composed photograph of A woman taken with a 50mm lens."`, `"wearing a blue shirt" ||| "wearing a red shirt"`) and projects out their top-3 SVD directions (`debias=True`, `coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/core.py:399-408`, `coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/core.py:457-461`). That adds 48 extra Qwen + refiner passes per build and projects out real camera (`50mm lens`), framing (`center/right`), and color (`blue/red`) directions.
- **Why `diff_span` (`h3_spans.py:530-550`) + `add_norm_matched` (`control_vectors.py:2013-2017`, `control_vectors.py:2114-2122`) is strictly better**:
  - Pooling only `[first_diff:end_diff]` removes shared prefix and suffix dilution directly from the BPE token IDs, placing all **64** shipped `.toml` `diff_span` vectors on disk (`32` banks $\times 2$ families; `90` total vectors on disk) into a tight `raw_norm` band of **`49.45 .. 87.03`** (mean `62.5`, `split_half_cosine` mean `0.923`) without synthetic nuisance SVD projection.

### 2.2 Inference Row Masking (`non_vision` / `scene_only` / `after_dialogue` vs. Unmasked `ContextShift`)
- In `MiniMaxH3Model._forward` (`comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:726-734`), `context` mixes `tag == 0` vision-pad rows (modulated by the **video adaLN stream**, `modality 0`) and `tag == 1` text rows (modulated by the **text adaLN stream**, `modality 1`).
- `MAINodes`' `ContextShift` (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/core.py:102`) adds `self.shift` to every row of `context`. In our `ref2va` ablation (`Arms 18 vs. 19` and `Arms 21 vs. 22` in [`bench/results/2026-11-05_control_vectors_pika_and_ref2va.md`](../../../../bench/results/2026-11-05_control_vectors_pika_and_ref2va.md)), shifting `tag == 0` vision rows caused a `13.80–15.48` pixel MAD shift in `<Subject 1>`'s mid-turn facial expression compared to `mask = "non_vision"`.
- Likewise, shifting `<d>...</d>` dialogue tokens corrupts phoneme pronunciation and lip-sync unless protected by `mask = "scene_only"` or `mask = "after_dialogue"`.

### 2.3 Checkpoint Family & Pika `token_refiner` LoRA Verification
- `MAINodes` stamps every dial with `"model_family": "MiniMax H3"` (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/core.py:57`, `coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/core.py:87-90`) and never distinguishes `fl2va` (`transformer`) from `ref2va` (`transformer_ref`).
- Yet `fl2va` and `ref2va` have completely different `condition_proj` and `token_refiner` weights: the cross-family cosine between an `fl2va` vector and a `ref2va` vector extracted from the exact same pairs is only **`~0.19`** ([`docs/wiki/control_vectors.md`](../../control_vectors.md)). A `MAINodes` dial built on `fl2va` silently loads onto a `ref2va` graph and injects a nearly orthogonal direction.
- Our `FAMILY_TENSOR_KEYS` and `KNOWN_FAMILIES` (`control_vectors.py:75-86`, `control_vectors.py:1265-1349`) and `_attach_refiner_lora_branches` (`control_vector_nodes.py:360-434`) verify the exact `fl2va` vs. `ref2va` vs. `fasth3` family hash and attach Pika's 24 `token_refiner.blocks.{0,1}` LoRA branches (`lora_2a8c2b52` for `fl2va`, `lora_865ee189` for `ref2va`), while `_lora_family_signature` (`control_vectors.py:2005-2046`) allows switching cleanly between Pika LoRA variants (`svd995`, `svd99`, `comfy_bf16`, `v1_avg_rank_56_bf16` / `v1_avg_rank_55_bf16` / `kijai_bf16`) of the same family when `applied` covers the full `token_refiner`. *(Updated 2026-11-06: previously listed only `kijai_bf16` without `v1_avg_rank_\d+_bf16` and did not check `applied` coverage.)*

### 2.4 Standalone `safe_open` Refiner Runner vs. Loading the 22 GB DiT
- `MAINodes`' `make_dial` (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/core.py:423`, `coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/core.py:581`) calls `comfy.model_management.load_models_gpu([model])`, forcing ComfyUI to load the 22 GB 50-block DiT into VRAM (and evict `Qwen3-VL-32B`) just to execute `condition_proj` + `token_refiner`.
- Our `build_refiner_runner` (`control_vector_nodes.py:429-510`) streams only `condition_proj.*` and `token_refiner.*` (`~0.7 GB`) via `safe_open` in `< 0.2s`, keeping `Qwen3-VL-32B` hot in VRAM across consecutive builds in `bench/control_vectors.py ui` and `build --all`.

### 2.5 Dead-End Seams (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/blocks.py` Per-Block Text Deltas & `condition_proj.diff_b` Pre-Refiner Bias LoRAs)
- `MAINodes`' own empirical log (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/LESSONS.md:39-44`, `coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/LESSONS.md:65-67`) confirmed that:
  1. Saving a vector as a pre-refiner `diffusion_model.condition_proj.diff_b` bias LoRA (`mode="pre"`, `coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/core.py:471-480`) fails to transfer across scenes because `token_refiner`'s 2 bidirectional self-attention blocks make a pre-refiner bias prompt-dependent (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/LESSONS.md:39-44`).
  2. Injecting per-block text deltas across the 50 DiT blocks (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/blocks.py:87-116`) found that blocks `0..24` do ~100% of the work, blocks `25..49` only add pixel jitter (`MAD 4.9`), and no block subset separated look from motion (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/LESSONS.md:65-67`).

## 3. Deep Architectural Analysis: What We Should Adopt & Adapt for Pika H3 Flex

### 3.1 Eliminating the Pain of Authoring 10 Full-Grammar Prompt Pairs

Why did our initial `.toml` banks require 10 full 3-section H3 prompts (~3,000 words)?
1. **Causal Stage 1 (`MiniMaxQwen3VL`, `comfy/text_encoders/minimax.py:89-108`)**: Tokens inside `[first_diff:end_diff]` only attend to tokens before them (`0..first_diff`). Without a realistic H3 shot header (`integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action...` or `ref2va` `subject_definitions:`), Qwen3-VL places the clause in a bare-caption subspace (`frame = "bare"` vs. `frame = "scene"` on `film_16mm_1974.toml`).
2. **Bidirectional Stage 2 (`TokenRefiner`, `comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:200, 250-277`)**: In the 2 refiner self-attention blocks (`mask=None`), tokens in `[first_diff:end_diff]` attend to both the preceding scene setup and the trailing `overall_soundscape:` / `non_diegetic_music: N/A` sections. Averaging over 10 diverse scenes cancels scene-specific context leakage.

We can eliminate hand-writing 10 full scenes through **two complementary mechanisms**:

#### Path A: Single-Clause / Short-Contrast Pair Compiler (`8 Visual + 2 Soundscape` Canonical Frame Bank)
- **Key insight**: Because `pooling = "diff_span"` (`h3_spans.py:530-550`) pools **only** `[first_diff:end_diff]`, the surrounding 3-section scene text is never averaged into the vector directly—it only provides causal prefix conditioning (`0..first_diff`) and bidirectional refiner context (`0..L`)!
- **How the compiler works**:
  - Maintain a **Stratified H3 Canonical Frame Bank** of 10 neutral, pre-validated 3-section H3 base scenes (8 visual scenes + 2 soundscape scenes, covering interior/exterior, male/female/object/two-person, `t2va` and `ref2va` `<Subject 1>`, speaking `<d>[English] ...</d>` and silent).
  - The user provides **only 1 to 3 short contrast clause pairs** `(pole_a, pole_b)` (~15–30 words total instead of ~3,000 words) plus an optional soundscape contrast `(sound_a, sound_b)` and selects one of **four grammatical slot templates**:
    1. `shot_look_clause`: slotted after `[Shot 1] Live-action, {CLAUSE}, ...` (camera, lens, film stock, lighting, color grade; independent of subject pronouns).
    2. `environment_physics_clause`: slotted as an adverbial/prepositional clause at the post-speech sentence boundary (gravity, weather, atmosphere, room state).
    3. `subject_action_clause`: uses `{Subj}` / `{subj}` / `{poss}` placeholders automatically bound to each base frame's subject (`("He", "he", "his")`, `("She", "she", "her")`, `("<Subject 1>", "<Subject 1>", "their")`).
    4. `vocal_or_soundscape_clause`: slotted into the speech delivery parenthetical before `<d>[English] ...</d>` or into `overall_soundscape:`.
- **Two Guardrails Verified Against `h3_spans.py` and `control_vectors.py`**:
  1. **Never edit both `integrated_multimodal_description:` and `overall_soundscape:` inside the same pair**: Because `h3_spans.py::differing_token_bounds` (`h3_spans.py:530-550`) computes a single contiguous span `[first_diff:end_diff]`, changing both sections in the same pair would bridge across the unchanged middle text. Putting visual clauses in pairs `1..8` and soundscape clauses in pairs `9..10` (our proven `8 Visual + 2 Soundscape` recipe from [`bench/results/2026-11-05_control_vectors_pika_and_ref2va.md`](../../../../bench/results/2026-11-05_control_vectors_pika_and_ref2va.md)) keeps every pair's `[first_diff:end_diff]` tight.
  2. **Lexical diversity & `n_unique_diff_spans` reporting**: If the exact same `(pole_a, pole_b)` string is slotted into all 10 frames, `control_vectors.py::lint_pair_set` (`control_vectors.py:864-876`) warns on duplicate spans and `mean_pairwise_cosine` measures context invariance (`~0.92–0.98`) rather than lexical invariance. Cycling 2–3 short synonymous clause variants across the 10 frames (still < 30 words total) and recording `n_unique_diff_spans` in `quality_report` (`control_vectors.py:1217-1234`) preserves both lexical diversity and honest metrics.

#### Path B: Media-Driven Control Vectors (`<Picture>` & `<Video>` Contrast via `Qwen3-VL` + `build_refiner_runner`)
- **How `<Picture i>`, `<Video k>`, and `<Audio j>` actually traverse `Qwen3-VL` and `token_refiner`**:
  - In `MiniMaxH3Tokenizer.tokenize_with_weights` (`comfy/text_encoders/minimax.py:197-252`) and `reference_conditioning.py:1245-1248`, reference items are prepended ahead of the prompt:
    - **Images (`<Picture i>`)**: `"<Picture %d>: "` (`tag == 1`) + `VISION_START` + Qwen3-VL vision patches + `VISION_END` (`tag == 0`, `comfy/text_encoders/minimax.py:79-86`).
    - **Videos (`<Video k>`)**: `"<Video %d>: "` (`tag == 1`) + 2 fps blocks of `"<%.1f seconds>"` (`tag == 1`) + `VISION_START` + 2-frame vision patches + `VISION_END` (`tag == 0`).
    - **Audio (`<Audio j>`)**: Emits **only** the 5 text tokens `"<Audio %d>: "` (`comfy/text_encoders/minimax.py:12`, `comfy/text_encoders/minimax.py:226-227`; `reference_conditioning.py:769-774`). **Architectural boundary**: Qwen3-VL has no audio tower in H3; audio waveforms go exclusively through `audio_vae` into `minimax_refs[i]["audio_latent"]`. Therefore, **post-refiner `5376`-d media vectors can be built from Pictures and Videos, never from Audio waveforms** (audio vectors must be built from text/soundscape clauses).
- **Bug in `MAINodes`' Picture/Video Pooling (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/core.py:596-599`) and How `h3_spans.py` Fixes It**:
  - `MAINodes` pools `refined[tags == 1].mean(0)`. However, by `h3_spans.py:243-295`, `tags == 1` includes the prefix label tokens `"<Picture 1>: "` **before** `VISION_START` (which under causal `Qwen3-VL` never saw the image at all!) and `"<0.0 seconds>"` timestamp tokens interleaved between video blocks.
  - Using our `h3_spans.py::spans(tokens, tags)["scene"]` (`h3_spans.py:293-305`) pools **strictly the post-vision scene tokens** that causally attended to the full image/video across all 50 Qwen3-VL layers and then bidirectionally cross-attended in `token_refiner` (with Pika's 24 `token_refiner` LoRA branches attached via `build_refiner_runner`).
  - Furthermore, because media-contrasted vectors on identical framing text have raw norms of `~23–30` (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/LESSONS.md:45`), our `mode = "add_norm_matched"` (`NORM_MATCHED_TARGET = 67.5`, `control_vectors.py:2114-2122`) automatically scales them to unit slider ergonomics (`strength = 1.0`).

### 3.2 DiT Velocity-Space Stream Split (`video_scale`, `audio_scale`) & Temporal Curves on Pika 6-Step LCM

#### Why Text-Token Masks Alone Cannot Prevent DiT Cross-Modal Coupling
In [`2026-11-06_detangle_comparison_and_borrowable_concepts.md`](2026-11-06_detangle_comparison_and_borrowable_concepts.md) (§3, Change 2), extending `h3_spans.py::inference_mask` (`h3_spans.py:350-382`) with `description_only` and `soundscape_only` is proposed to separate visual and audio steering at the text-token level. While adding those masks is worthwhile for prompts with long soundscape sections, **text-token masking alone cannot stop cross-modal coupling inside the DiT**:
- In `MiniMaxH3Model._embed_and_pack` and `_forward` (`comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:591-604`, `comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:760-773`), all 50 `DiTBlock`s run **full unmasked joint self-attention** over one contiguous sequence `h = [text, cond, ref_img, ref_audio, audio, video]`.
- Every `audio` latent token attends directly to `integrated_multimodal_description:` text rows and to `video` latent rows.
- We already measured this in `Arm 30` and `Arm 36` ([`bench/results/2026-11-05_control_vectors_pika_and_ref2va.md`](../../../../bench/results/2026-11-05_control_vectors_pika_and_ref2va.md)): in `prompt_bank/control_vectors/head_swell.toml:1-140`, all 10 pairs have **100% identical `overall_soundscape:` sections** (`zero` soundscape diff tokens), yet steering `head_swell` still shifted the speaker's vocal pitch up **`+67%` (`131.2 -> 219.2 Hz`)** and added loud pressurized steam foley (`RMS = 0.0143 -> 0.1134`) because the DiT's audio stream attends to `"inflates into an oversized ballooning cranium"` in the visual description!
- Conversely, in `Arm 47` (`operatic_bellow`), the DiT's video stream attends to the vocal tokens and makes the porter throw his head back with his jaw stretched wide.

#### Exact Velocity-Blend Contract in `WrappersMP.DIFFUSION_MODEL` (`comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:606-647`, `comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:792`)
The only point where video and audio are cleanly separated is at the output of `MiniMaxH3Model._forward` (`comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:792`), which returns the 2-element list:

$$\text{out} = \bigl[-\text{out}_{\text{video}}\in\mathbb{R}^{1\times 24\times T_{\text{lat}}\times H_{\text{lat}}\times W_{\text{lat}}}, \quad -\text{out}_{\text{audio}}\in\mathbb{R}^{1\times 32\times 2\times T_{\text{aud}}}\bigr]$$

1. **Exact commutation with `ModelSamplingAV`'s outer audio carry (`comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:643-646`)**:
   - After `WrappersMP.DIFFUSION_MODEL` returns `out`, `MiniMaxH3Model.forward` (`comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:643-646`) converts `out[1]` to the carried audio variable via the **affine** map:
     $$\text{Carry}(\text{out}[1]) = (1 - \text{scale}) (\text{src}_{\text{audio}}\cdot\text{carry}) + \bigl(1 + (\text{scale} - 1)\sigma_a\bigr) \text{out}[1]$$
   - Because $\text{Carry}(\cdot)$ is strictly affine, any linear velocity blend $\text{out}_{\text{audio}} = \mathbf{v}_{\text{base}}[1] + w_{\text{audio}} (\mathbf{v}_{\text{steered}}[1] - \mathbf{v}_{\text{base}}[1])$ computed inside our `DIFFUSION_MODEL` wrapper commutes **identically** with `forward()`'s outer carry transform.
2. **Economics on Pika 6-Step LCM (`cfg = 1.0`)**:
   - Stock H3 (`50` steps, `cfg = 4.0`) runs `100` DiT forwards. Pika H3 Flex (`6` steps, `cfg = 1.0`) runs only **`6` single forwards** (no unconditional CFG pass).
   - Running a 2-forward velocity split (`v_base` with `context_base`, `v_steered` with `context_steered`) on only the **first 2 steps** (`steps 0..1`, `split_end_percent = 0.35`, where Pika commits layout, physics, and vocal pitch) costs `6 + 2 = 8` DiT forwards (**`+33%` DiT compute**, ~`+14s`), and costs **zero extra forwards** whenever `video_scale == audio_scale` and no temporal curve is active.
3. **Why Combining Our Token Masks (`scene_only` / `description_only`) + Velocity Split (`audio_scale = 0.0`) Beats `MAINodes`**:
   - In `coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/LESSONS.md:126-127`, `coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/LESSONS.md:146-149`, setting `audio="protect"` ($w_{\text{audio}} = 0$) alone still allowed ~20–30% of coupling on step $s \ge 1$ because `MAINodes` shifted `<d>...</d>` dialogue rows in `context` (`all_rows`) and the packed DiT sequence re-coupled them on subsequent steps.
   - Combining **our token-level masks** (`scene_only` / `after_dialogue` / `description_only`, keeping `<d>...</d>` and `overall_soundscape:` text rows unshifted) **with** `audio_scale = 0.0` velocity splitting on steps `0..1` blocks both the text-token pathway and the early-step audio velocity pathway simultaneously.
4. **Preserving H3's `1, 4, 4, 4, 4` Clock & Fixing Linear Extrapolation Outside $M(t) \in [0, 1]$ (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/timecurve.py:114-131`, `coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/timecurve.py:168-173`)**:
   - `MAINodes`' `clip_clock` and `frame_weights` (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/timecurve.py:114-131`) properly average the curve $f(t)$ across each video latent token's pixel-frame span under `FRAME_PER_TOKEN = (1, 4, 4, 4, 4)` (`comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:30`, `comfy/model_base.py:2297-2308`: every 5 latent frames = `1 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 = 17` pixel frames at 24 fps) and evaluate audio tokens at `AUDIO_LATENT_FPS = 40` Hz.
   - However, because `TimeCurveForward.__call__` (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/timecurve.py:168-173`) blends only two branches (`v0 + M(t) * (v_dial - v0)`), weights $M(t) \notin [0, 1]$ linearly extrapolate in velocity space rather than evaluating the DiT at a negative or amplified strength (`coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/dials/LESSONS.md:114-118`). Our implementation should preserve the `FRAME_PER_TOKEN` pixel-frame-range averaging contract and constrain each branch's envelope weight to $M(t) \in [0, 1]$ (evaluating distinct signed/amplified branches directly in `context`).

### 3.3 Native H3 `ref2va` 3D VAE Reference Gate (`ref_vae_end_percent` & Per-Reference Softening) Without Core Patches

When `MiniMaxH3ReferenceConditioning` (`reference_conditioning.py:1180-1280`) or `MiniMaxH3EncodeReferences` (`reference_encode.py:440-490`) runs on a `ref2va` graph, `<Picture 1>` enters the DiT through **two parallel pathways**:
1. **Pathway A (Qwen3-VL Vision Tokens, `tag == 0` in `context`)**: ~400–900 semantic vision tokens modulated by video adaLN (`modality 0`, `comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:726-734`).
2. **Pathway B (3D Video VAE Reference Latents, `minimax_payload["cond_video_latents"]`)**: `7,360` 3D VAE patch tokens injected on every sampling step via `_cond_video_rows` (`comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:535-548`):
   ```python
   aug = payload.get("visual_cond_noise_aug", VISUAL_COND_TIMESTEP)  # 0.999
   seed = int(payload.get("seed", 0))
   for z in payload.get("cond_video_latents", []):
       r = patchify_video(z.to(torch.float32), self.patch_size)
       if aug < 1.0:
           gen = torch.Generator("cpu").manual_seed(seed)
           noise = torch.randn(r.shape, generator=gen, dtype=torch.float32)
           r = aug * r + (1.0 - aug) * noise.to(r.device)
   ```
   with segment timestep `seg_t["ref_img"] = max(t_v, vis_aug)` (`comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:682`).

Because `aug = 0.999` injects nearly noise-free 3D VAE latents of `<Picture 1>` across all 6 Pika steps, strong material/style vectors (`felt_muppet` in `Arm 21`, `rubber_hose_1930s` in `Arm 50`) stylize the background street and performance while `<Subject 1>` remains photorealistic live-action.

While `MAINodes`' `coderef/ComfyUI-MAINodes/h3_cond_strength.py:324-350` monkey-patched `MiniMaxH3Model._cond_video_rows` on the class (which violates `AGENTS.md` and `bench/check_vsa_core_patch.py`), we control Pathway B with **zero ComfyUI core patches** inside our `WrappersMP.DIFFUSION_MODEL` wrapper (`control_vector_nodes.py:1144-1158`):
1. **`ref_vae_end_percent` (Zero 3D VAE `cond_video_latents` on Late Pika Steps While Preserving `PackedLayout` RoPE `cursor`)**:
   - On steps `0..2` (`progress <= ref_vae_end_percent`, e.g., `0.50` in `Arm 66`), pass `minimax_payload` unchanged so the `7,360` 3D VAE reference tokens lock `<Subject 1>`'s identity, wardrobe, and framing.
   - On late steps (`progress > ref_vae_end_percent`), pass a shallow copy of `minimax_payload` that keeps `payload["refs"]` and `payload["layout"]` (`seq_len`) untouched—because dropping `refs` and rebuilding `PackedLayout` mid-trajectory shifts the 3D RoPE `cursor` for all subsequent `audio` and `video` tokens (`Arm 66` in [`bench/results/2026-11-05_control_vectors_pika_and_ref2va.md:172`](../../../../bench/results/2026-11-05_control_vectors_pika_and_ref2va.md#L172))—while setting `visual_cond_noise_aug = 0.0` and zeroing `cond_video_latents` (and keeping Qwen3-VL's `tag == 0` vision tokens in `context`).
   - **Result (`Arm 66`)**: Frees material/style control vectors (`felt_muppet`, `claymation_stopmotion`, `rubber_hose_1930s`) to finish stylizing `<Subject 1>`'s surface texture on late steps without shifting the 3D RoPE `cursor`.
2. **Per-Reference Latent Pre-Blending (`ref_vae_aug`)**:
   - Because `patchify_video` (`comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:42-47`, `comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:542`) is a linear tensor permutation (`patchify_video(a * z + (1 - a) * n) == a * patchify_video(z) + (1 - a) * patchify_video(n)`), pre-blending `r["latent"]` on a cloned `minimax_refs` entry before `_cond_video_rows` runs softens `<Picture 1>`'s 3D VAE copy-lock while leaving `minimax_keyframes` guides at `0.999`—with zero monkey-patching of `MiniMaxH3Model`.
