# The periphery: how it differs, aligns, and what it does that others do not

last updated: 2026-11-22 (DiffSynth's training now holds references at 0.999 and trains on the six knots; LightX2V's H3-Image refiner runs once; Pika's training answer; templates at 16 MP; new community rows; [`sweeps/2026-11-22.md`](sweeps/2026-11-22.md)); 2026-11-17 (DiffSynth's captions fix and training resolution; DiffSynth and LightX2V H3-Image inference paths read stage by stage, [`topics/h3_image_pipeline.md`](topics/h3_image_pipeline.md); the t2i vision-patch idea set against the engines); 2026-11-16 (added `geiru_context_suite`, `NerdNotes`, `ComfyUI-MMH3Tools`, and `ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3-Context-Loop` from hand audit); 2026-11-15 (page created; state as of [`sweeps/2026-11-15.md`](sweeps/2026-11-15.md))

Everything outside sglang, vllm-omni, diffusers and core: the other libraries, distill releases, node packs,
Hugging Face uploads and papers. **Periphery is never a vote** ([`index.md`](index.md), "When upstreams disagree").
It is read for where it differs from the ranked engines, where it aligns with them, and what it does that none of
them do. **Community custom nodes are community research:** interesting, trusted for nothing until something of ours
checks them, and their numbers are their claims.

This page holds the standing picture per source. Each sweep record has the dated detail.
