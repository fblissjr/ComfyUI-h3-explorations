2. Why LLM Steering Intuition Breaks on DiTs (Frozen Anchor vs. Self-Steering KV)

Root cause of 1D failure & ColBERT collapse

In an autoregressive LLM, nudging logits changes what gets appended to the growing KV cache. Inside H3, the prompt table never updates—every DiT block re-reads the exact same contradiction until you explicitly remove it.

Double-Dissociation Proof (Locked RMS = 101.25): At byte-identical perturbation norm, noir's own V_r flipped 5 / 5 seeds to true B&W (hsv_sat = 0.022–0.026), whereas swapping in paper_origami's V_r, a random V_r, or generic PCs produced 0 / 5 B&W. On paper_origami, 1D deleted the daughter and wrong erasers melted the porter, while paper_origami's own V_r preserved 5 / 5 folded-paper actors. Subspace erasure is genuine regression hygiene — not ColBERT duality.

The vgel.me Conditional Bridge

In Making Neurons by Hand, wiring gate_proj = up_proj = read inside a SwiGLU MLP gives silu(z) · z — matching why Swift 1.5 concentrates its LoRA deltas in mlp.gate_proj and mlp.up_proj.

Mathematical catch: Because negative × negative is positive, silu(z)·z leaks a +0.557 bump onto opposite/clean inputs. Explicit ReLU deadzone gating on cos > 0.10 eliminates that leak while driving clean H3 prompts down 38x–50x (1.36–1.77 vs. 67.50 RMS).
