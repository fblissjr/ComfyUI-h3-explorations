Why unpooled fp32 __spans (~8.6 MB/stem) and the 48/50 overall_soundscape fix were non-negotiable

1. Never pre-pool stems to [N_pairs, 2, 5376]: A pooled 2-vector summary per pair collapses per-token softmax similarity (tau = 0.15) down to scalar [[1.0]] and forces SVD on a typical 6..8-pair stem to be rank-deficient (rank ≤ 7 < 8), silently destroying Rank-8 Pole-A erasure (erasure.vr). Unpooled fp32 token spans (~8.6 MB/stem) keep true full-rank subspace geometry intact while preserving strict < 1e-6 parity across all 143 bank files.

2. Fixing the silent audio blind spot across 48/50 stems: Auditing the bank revealed llm_pairs.py had been dropping overall_soundscape edits on generation, leaving 48/50 legacy stems text-only on visual buses. Restoring paired soundscape deltas inside [routing] turned on multi-bus coupling—producing reverb_cathedral (+22.3% acoustic tail), synchronized heavy_blizzard wind/gale cues, and 1920s silent-film + cranked projector click beds in one pass.

3. Why LLM Steering Intuition Breaks on DiTs (Frozen Anchor vs. Self-Steering KV)

Root cause of 1D failure & ColBERT collapse

In an autoregressive LLM, nudging logits changes what gets appended to the growing KV cache. Inside H3, the prompt table never updates—every DiT block re-reads the exact same contradiction until you explicitly remove it.

Historical Double-Dissociation Proof (Locked RMS = 101.25): At byte-identical perturbation norm, noir's own V_r flipped 5 / 5 seeds to true B&W (hsv_sat = 0.022–0.026), whereas swapping in paper_origami's V_r, a random V_r, or generic PCs produced 0 / 5 B&W. On paper_origami, 1D deleted the daughter and wrong erasers melted the porter, while paper_origami's own V_r preserved 5 / 5 folded-paper actors. Subspace erasure is genuine regression hygiene — and today powers heavy_blizzard, reverb_cathedral (+22.3%), and coupled [routing] stems cleanly.
