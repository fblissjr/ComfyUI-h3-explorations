## 1. Dissecting the "Magic Math Equation" (`Theorem 1`) & Two Core Algorithms: Prior Art vs. Net-New

### 1.1 What `Theorem 1` Actually Means in Plain English

On `2026-11-20`, the initial unpooled slider prototype (`M3_subspace`) fit a rank-$r$ linear ridge regression $\mathbf{W}_D$ mapping centered Pole-A token states $\mathbf{A}_c$ to matched token shifts $\mathbf{D}_c = \mathbf{B}_c - \mathbf{A}_c$:

$$\mathbf{W}_D = \arg\min_{\operatorname{rank}(\mathbf{W}) \le r} \|\mathbf{D}_c - \mathbf{A}_c \mathbf{W}\|_F^2 + \lambda \|\mathbf{W}\|_F^2 = (\mathbf{A}_c^\top \mathbf{A}_c + \lambda \mathbf{I})^{-1} \mathbf{A}_c^\top (\mathbf{B}_c - \mathbf{A}_c)$$

Initially, the session assumed `M3` flipped `noir` and preserved `paper_origami` figures because $\mathbf{W}_D$ learned a rich multi-facet **Pole-B mapping** from input tokens to output style tokens. Substituting the thin SVD $\mathbf{A}_c = \mathbf{U}_r \boldsymbol{\Sigma}_r \mathbf{V}_r^\top$ into the normal equations splits $\mathbf{W}_D$ algebraically (`Theorem 1`, [`bench/verify_pole_a_erasure.py`](../../../../bench/verify_pole_a_erasure.py), exact to `5.86e-14`):

$$(\mathbf{h} - \boldsymbol{\mu}_A)\mathbf{W}_D \;\equiv\; \underbrace{(\mathbf{h} - \boldsymbol{\mu}_A)\mathbf{V}_r^\top \operatorname{diag}\!\left(\frac{\sigma_i}{\sigma_i^2 + \lambda}\right)\mathbf{U}_r^\top \mathbf{B}_c}_{\mathcal{W}_B(\mathbf{h})\;\text{(Pole-B cross-covariance)}} \;-\; \underbrace{(\mathbf{h} - \boldsymbol{\mu}_A)\mathbf{V}_r^\top \operatorname{diag}\!\left(\frac{\sigma_i^2}{\sigma_i^2 + \lambda}\right)\mathbf{V}_r}_{\mathbf{P}_{\mathbf{V}_r,\lambda}(\mathbf{h})\;\text{(Tikhonov projection onto Pole A)}}$$

**Plain-English reality check:**
- **The algebra is NOT a new discovery.** Distributing $(\mathbf{A}^\top \mathbf{A} + \lambda \mathbf{I})^{-1} \mathbf{A}^\top$ across $(\mathbf{B} - \mathbf{A})$ is textbook linear algebra (Tikhonov ridge shrinkage of $-\mathbf{A}$ onto $\mathbf{A}$). It holds for **any** matrix $\mathbf{B}$ — ColBERT has nothing to do with the $-\mathbf{P}_{\mathbf{V}_r,\lambda}$ term! Indeed, [`steering/extract.py:176-201`](../../../../control_vectors.py#L1333-L1358) computes `erasure.vr` and `erasure.diag` **purely from `a_spans` without touching `b_spans` at all**.
- **What WAS genuinely valuable and validated by independent verification** was the *empirical diagnostic* revealed when we evaluated both terms on H3's `5376`-D `TokenRefiner` states: because diverse Pole-A scenes are uncorrelated with a fixed Pole-B concept, $\|\mathcal{W}_B\|$ was `4.2x–10.3x` smaller than $\|\mathbf{P}_{\mathbf{V}_r,\lambda}\|$, proving **`97%–99.6%`** of `M3`'s runtime shift was simply **deleting Pole A's conflicting subspace**! Dropping $\mathcal{W}_B$, switching from global $\boldsymbol{\mu}_A$ to prompt-local $\bar{\mathbf{h}}$ centering, adding soft-MaxSim $\hat{\boldsymbol{\mu}}_d$, clamping erasure to $\operatorname{clip}(s, 0, 1)$, orthogonalizing holds, and RMS-matching to $|s|\cdot 67.5$ gave **Algorithm 1** (offline `193.5 KB` extraction) and **Algorithm 2** (online `1x` Prelude EQ).
