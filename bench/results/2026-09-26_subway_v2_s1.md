# The rewritten subway prompt on four models, 2026-09-26

**A first look, not a verdict**, and not an A/B of the prompt change: a
rendered clip at one seed cannot measure a prompt's effect
(`docs/eval_comparison.md`). It shows what each model does with the new text.
The manifest is `bench/subway_v2_arms.json`, and the rows are
`2026-09-26_subway_v2_s1.jsonl`.

**The prompt:** `t2va_subway_chase` as rewritten in `18bd00ca`. It states two
people, one path (turnstiles, then stairs down, then the platform), and an
agent for every action.

**The models:**
- the undistilled base: `h3_text_to_video`, 16 steps;
- PDD8: `h3_text_to_video_pdd`;
- FlashGen: `h3_text_to_video_flashgen`, rank 64 applied at the call;
- FastH3: `h3_probe_t2v_fasth3_8step_contract`.

**Setup:** seed 730451892, 1344x768, 345 frames, decoded with the INT8 video
VAE (0.151.0).

## By eye, not a ranking

Four frames per arm, at frames 24, 120, 216 and 312.

- **All four models render the same four beats**, the prompt's plan:
  1. the suspect vaulting the turnstile under a "DOWNTOWN PLATFORM" sign;
  2. the agent on the stairwell with a hand at his ear;
  3. a wide low-angle shot up the stairs, with two figures coming down;
  4. the tackle on the platform floor.
- **No arm puts anyone on an escalator.** In each sampled frame the cast count
  is two or fewer.
- **Against the old text at the same seed**
  (`2026-09-26_distill_compare_s1.md`):
  - FlashGen had its runner on an escalator going the wrong way;
  - PDD8's third shot was an empty corridor;
  - the owner saw a cloned pursuer around 5 s in FlashGen and FastH3.

  This run's frames do not show those, but four frames per clip cannot rule
  out a clone between them. The owner's full watch is the check.
